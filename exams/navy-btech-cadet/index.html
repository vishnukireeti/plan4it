Navy 10+2 B.Tech Cadet Entry

Navy 10+2 B.Tech Cadet Entry is conducted by Indian Navy. It is used for admission to B.Tech and officer in Indian Navy.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: joinindiannavy.gov.in
Conducted by
Indian Navy
Eligibility
Class 12 (PCM) with JEE Main score
Courses
B.Tech and officer in Indian Navy
Mode
JEE Main score plus SSB interview
Difficulty
Very High

Preparation tip

Prepare for the written test and the fitness and medical standards at the same time.
