IIMC Entrance (Journalism)

IIMC Entrance (Journalism) is conducted by Indian Institute of Mass Communication (Deemed to be University). It is used for admission to PG diploma in journalism.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: iimc.gov.in
Conducted by
Indian Institute of Mass Communication (Deemed to be University)
Eligibility
PG Diplomas: any degree. MA: any degree with 55%
Courses
PG diploma in journalism
Mode
Admission through CUET PG (NTA); no separate IIMC entrance test
Age limit
PG Diplomas: up to 25 (OBC 28, SC/ST/PwD 30) on 1 Aug 2026. MA: no age limit
Difficulty
High
