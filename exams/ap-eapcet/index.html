AP EAPCET

AP EAPCET is conducted by JNTU Kakinada (on behalf of APSCHE). It is used for admission to B.Tech / B.Pharm / Agri in Andhra Pradesh.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: cets.apsche.ap.gov.in
Conducted by
JNTU Kakinada (on behalf of APSCHE)
Eligibility
Class 12 (MPC/BiPC) with 45% aggregate
Courses
B.Tech / B.Pharm / Agri in Andhra Pradesh
Mode

3 hours

High
