CMAT

CMAT is conducted by National Testing Agency (NTA). It is used for admission to MBA at AICTE-approved B-schools.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: cmat.nta.nic.in
Conducted by
National Testing Agency (NTA)
Eligibility
Bachelor's degree in any discipline (final-year students can apply)
Courses
MBA at AICTE-approved B-schools
Mode
Online (CBT), English only
Duration
3 hours
Attempts
One attempt per year
Age limit
No age limit
Difficulty
Medium

Application fee

General: ₹2,500 (male), ₹1,250 (female). EWS/SC/ST/OBC-NCL/PwBD/third gender: ₹1,250

From the official 2026 notice.

Exam pattern

100 MCQs, 400 marks in 5 sections (Quant and DI, Logical Reasoning, Language, General Awareness, Innovation and Entrepreneurship). +4 correct, −1 wrong

Upcoming dates

Exam7 Feb 2027 (proposed)
StatusTentative

These dates are expected, not yet announced. Dates move. Confirm on the official website before you plan around them. The app shows the latest dates and can remind you before registration closes.

How competitive it is

~70,000 applicants — illustrative. Dates tentative unless marked confirmed — verify on the official site.

Preparation tip

Wide GK/Innovation section; easier than CAT so accuracy across all 100 questions matters.
