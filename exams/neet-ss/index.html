NEET SS

NEET SS is conducted by National Board of Examinations in Medical Sciences (NBEMS). It is used for admission to DM and MCh super specialty.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: natboard.edu.in
Conducted by
National Board of Examinations in Medical Sciences (NBEMS)
Eligibility
MD/MS/DNB in a feeder specialty (or due by 31 Jan 2027) with NMC or State Medical Council registration
Courses
DM and MCh super specialty
Mode
Online (CBT), morning or afternoon shift
Difficulty
Very High

Application fee

₹3,500 per group (no GST)

From the official 2026 notice.

Upcoming dates

Exam11 and 12 Dec 2026
StatusOfficial

Dates move. Confirm on the official website before you plan around them. The app shows the latest dates and can remind you before registration closes.

Preparation tip

Revise NCERT biology and chemistry, or your clinical subjects for PG exams. Practise mock tests.
