SNAP

SNAP is conducted by Symbiosis International (Deemed University). It is used for admission to MBA at Symbiosis institutes.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: snaptest.org
Conducted by
Symbiosis International (Deemed University)
Eligibility
Bachelor's degree with 50% (45% SC/ST)
Courses
MBA at Symbiosis institutes
Mode
Online (CBT) at test centres
Duration
60 minutes
Attempts
Up to 3 tests; the best score counts
Difficulty
Medium

Application fee

₹2,550 per test + ₹1,000 per programme (plus taxes)

From the official 2026 notice.

Exam pattern

60 MCQs, 60 marks: English 10, Reasoning 20, Quant/DI 20, Ethics and values 10. −25% per wrong answer

How competitive it is

~1 lakh applicants — illustrative. Dates tentative unless marked confirmed — verify on the official site.

Preparation tip

Only 60 questions in 60 minutes — speed and section balance matter more than depth.
