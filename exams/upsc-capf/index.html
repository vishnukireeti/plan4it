UPSC CAPF (Assistant Commandant)

UPSC CAPF (Assistant Commandant) is conducted by Union Public Service Commission (UPSC). It is used for admission to Officer in BSF, CRPF, CISF, ITBP, SSB.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: upsc.gov.in
Conducted by
Union Public Service Commission (UPSC)
Eligibility
Bachelor's degree; men and women. Physical standards apply (e.g. height 165 cm men, 157 cm women)
Courses
Officer in BSF, CRPF, CISF, ITBP, SSB
Mode
Written, then physical tests, interview and medical
Duration
Paper I 2 hours; Paper II 3 hours
Age limit
20 to 25 on 1 Aug of the exam year. Relaxation: SC/ST 5 years, OBC 3
Difficulty
Very High

Application fee

₹200; no fee for women and SC/ST candidates

From the official 2026 notice.

Exam pattern

Paper I General Ability and Intelligence (250, objective, one-third negative marking). Paper II GS, Essay and Comprehension (200, written)

Exam19 Jul 2026
ResultWritten result 25 Aug 2026

Preparation tip

Prepare for the written test and the fitness and medical standards at the same time.
