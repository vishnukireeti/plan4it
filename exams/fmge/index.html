FMGE (Foreign Medical Graduate Exam)

FMGE (Foreign Medical Graduate Exam) is conducted by National Board of Examinations in Medical Sciences (NBEMS). It is used for admission to Licence to practise in India.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: natboard.edu.in
Conducted by
National Board of Examinations in Medical Sciences (NBEMS)
Eligibility
Indian citizen or OCI with a foreign primary medical qualification recognised in that country; NMC eligibility certificate or NEET UG as required
Courses
Licence to practise in India
Mode
Online (CBT)
Attempts
Held twice a year (June and October sessions in 2026)
Difficulty
High

Application fee

₹4,650 (₹3,940.68 + 18% GST)

From the official October 2026 notice.
