CLAT UG

CLAT UG is conducted by Consortium of National Law Universities. It is used for admission to 5-year BA LLB (Hons) at 24 NLUs.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: consortiumofnlus.ac.in
Conducted by
Consortium of National Law Universities
Eligibility
Class 12 (any stream) with 45% (40% reserved)
Courses
5-year BA LLB (Hons) at 24 NLUs
Mode
Offline (pen and paper)
Duration
2 hours (2–4 PM); 2 hours 40 minutes for eligible PwD candidates
Attempts
No cap on attempts
Difficulty
Very High

Application fee

₹4,000 (General/OBC); ₹3,500 (SC/ST/BPL/PwD)

From the official 2027 notice.

How competitive it is

~65,000 applicants for ~3,000 NLU seats (~5%) — illustrative. Original Oct 31 2026 deadline was extended to Nov 7 2026 by the Consortium — confirm on the official site, as extensions can move again.

Preparation tip

Editorials and current affairs daily; legal reasoning passages are the biggest score driver.
