JEE Main & Advanced

JEE Main & Advanced is conducted by NTA / IITs. It is used for admission to B.Tech / B.E. / B.Arch.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: jeemain.nta.nic.in
Conducted by
NTA / IITs
Eligibility
Class 12 (PCM) with 75%
Courses
B.Tech / B.E. / B.Arch
Mode
Online (CBT), two sessions (Jan & Apr)
Duration
3 hours
Attempts
Up to 2 attempts/year (best score counted), within 3 years of Class 12
Difficulty
Very High

Application fee

₹1,000 (Gen, online) / ₹500 (SC/ST/PwD)

Dates move. Confirm on the official website before you plan around them. The app shows the latest dates and can remind you before registration closes.

How competitive it is

~12 lakh applicants for ~1.2 lakh engineering seats nationwide (~10% overall) — illustrative.

Preparation tip

Master NCERT fundamentals first — most JEE Main questions test conceptual clarity, not just speed.
