JEE Main Paper 2 (B.Arch / B.Planning)

JEE Main Paper 2 (B.Arch / B.Planning) is conducted by NTA. It is used for admission to B.Arch / B.Planning at NITs, SPAs, CFTIs.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: jeemain.nta.nic.in
Conducted by
NTA
Eligibility
Class 12 with Maths and 50% aggregate
Courses
B.Arch / B.Planning at NITs, SPAs, CFTIs
Mode
Online (Maths, Aptitude) + Offline (Drawing)
Duration
3 hours 30 min
Attempts
Up to 2 attempts/year, within 3 years of Class 12
Difficulty
High

Application fee

₹1,000 (Gen) / ₹500 (SC/ST/PwD) (approx)

Upcoming dates

Exam24 Jan 2027

Dates move. Confirm on the official website before you plan around them. The app shows the latest dates and can remind you before registration closes.

How competitive it is

~1 lakh applicants for ~5,000 seats — illustrative. Dates tentative unless marked confirmed — verify on the official site.

Preparation tip

Drawing is offline and pass/fail on quality; practise freehand sketches daily.
