IIT JAM

IIT JAM is conducted by IITs (JAM 2026 organised by IIT Bombay). It is used for admission to M.Sc, Joint M.Sc-PhD at IITs and IISc.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: jam.iitm.ac.in
Conducted by
IITs (JAM 2026 organised by IIT Bombay)
Eligibility
Bachelor's degree (final-year students can apply)
Courses
M.Sc, Joint M.Sc-PhD at IITs and IISc
Mode
Online (CBT)
Duration
3 hours per paper
Age limit
No age limit
Difficulty
Very High

Application fee

One paper: ₹2,000 (₹1,000 for women, SC/ST, PwD). Two papers: ₹2,700 (₹1,350)

From the official 2026 notice.

Exam pattern

60 questions, 100 marks: 30 MCQs (negative marking), 10 multiple-select, 20 numerical (no negative marking). 7 papers: Biotech, Chemistry, Economics, Geology, Maths, Mathematical Statistics, Physics

Last cycle (2026)

Registration5 Sep – 12 Oct 2025
Exam15 Feb 2026

The next dates are not out yet. Last year's timing is a rough guide only.

Preparation tip

Master the Class 11-12 and first-year BSc syllabus of your subject.
