UPSC Indian Statistical Service

UPSC Indian Statistical Service is conducted by Union Public Service Commission (UPSC). It is used for admission to Statistician in govt.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: upsc.gov.in
Conducted by
Union Public Service Commission (UPSC)
Eligibility
ISS: bachelor's with Statistics (or master's in Statistics). IES: master's in Economics, Applied, Business Economics or Econometrics
Courses
Statistician in govt
Mode
Written (1,000 marks), then interview (200)
Age limit
21 to 30 on 1 Aug of the exam year, with relaxations
Difficulty
Very High

Application fee

₹200; no fee for women, SC, ST and PwBD candidates

From the official 2026 notice.

Exam pattern

ISS: General English, General Studies, Statistics I–IV (I and II objective). IES: General English, General Studies, Economics I–III, Indian Economics
