NATA

NATA is conducted by Council of Architecture (CoA). It is used for admission to B.Arch at all CoA-approved colleges.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: nata.in
Conducted by
Council of Architecture (CoA)
Eligibility
Class 12 with PCM and 50% aggregate
Courses
B.Arch at all CoA-approved colleges
Mode
Offline (drawing) + Online (aptitude)
Duration
3 hours
Attempts
Up to 3 attempts a year; best counted
Difficulty
Medium

Application fee

₹2,000 (approx)

Upcoming dates

Exam11 Apr 2027

Dates move. Confirm on the official website before you plan around them. The app shows the latest dates and can remind you before registration closes.

How competitive it is

~50,000 applicants for ~10,000 seats — illustrative. Dates tentative unless marked confirmed — verify on the official site.

Preparation tip

Sketch daily: perspective, 3D composition and colour theory decide drawing marks.
