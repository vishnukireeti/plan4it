ITI Admission (state-wise)

ITI Admission (state-wise) is conducted by State Directorates of Training / DGT. It is used for admission to ITI trades: electrician, fitter, welder, COPA and more.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: dgt.gov.in
Conducted by
State Directorates of Training / DGT
Eligibility
Class 8 or 10 depending on trade
Courses
ITI trades: electrician, fitter, welder, COPA and more
Mode
Varies by state (mostly marks-based)
Difficulty
Medium

Preparation tip

Apply on your state ITI admission portal.
