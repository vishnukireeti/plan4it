UCEED

UCEED is conducted by IIT Bombay. It is used for admission to B.Des at IIT Bombay, Delhi, Guwahati, Hyderabad, IIITDM Jabalpur.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: uceed.iitb.ac.in
Conducted by
IIT Bombay
Eligibility
Class 12 (any stream)
Courses
B.Des at IIT Bombay, Delhi, Guwahati, Hyderabad, IIITDM Jabalpur
Mode
Offline (drawing + aptitude)
Duration
3 hours
Attempts
Up to 2 attempts (consecutive years)
Difficulty
Very High

Application fee

₹3,500 (Gen) / ₹1,750 (SC/ST/PwD/Female) (approx)

From the official 2027 notice.

Dates move. Confirm on the official website before you plan around them. The app shows the latest dates and can remind you before registration closes.

How competitive it is

~15,000 applicants for ~250 seats — illustrative. Dates tentative unless marked confirmed — verify on the official site.

Preparation tip

Part A tests visualisation and observation; sketch objects and scenes daily.
