NEST

NEST is conducted by NISER Bhubaneswar and UM-DAE CEBS Mumbai. It is used for admission to 5-year Integrated M.Sc at NISER and CEBS.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: nestexam.in
Conducted by
NISER Bhubaneswar and UM-DAE CEBS Mumbai
Eligibility
Class 12 with 60% (55% SC/ST/PwD) and three of Biology, Chemistry, Maths, Physics; passed in the last two years or appearing
Courses
5-year Integrated M.Sc at NISER and CEBS
Mode
Online (CBT)
Duration
3 hours (2–5 PM)
Attempts
Multiple attempts within age limit
Age limit
No age limit
Difficulty
Very High

Application fee

₹1,400 (General/OBC men); ₹700 (EWS, SC/ST, PwD and all women)

From the official 2026 notice.

Exam pattern

4 sections (Biology, Chemistry, Maths, Physics), 20 MCQs each, 60 marks each; best 3 count. +3 correct, −1 wrong

Upcoming dates

Exam13 Jun 2027

Dates move. Confirm on the official website before you plan around them. The app shows the latest dates and can remind you before registration closes.

How competitive it is

~15,000 applicants for ~350 seats — illustrative. Dates tentative unless marked confirmed — verify on the official site.

Preparation tip

Conceptual, negative marking on some sections; strong Class 11–12 fundamentals are the key.
