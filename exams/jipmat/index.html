JIPMAT

JIPMAT is conducted by National Testing Agency (NTA) for IIM Bodh Gaya and IIM Jammu. It is used for admission to IPM at IIM Jammu & IIM Bodh Gaya.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: exams.nta.nic.in
Conducted by
National Testing Agency (NTA) for IIM Bodh Gaya and IIM Jammu
Eligibility
Class 12 (any stream) passed in the last two years or appearing; Class 10 passed in or after the year set in the bulletin
Courses
IPM at IIM Jammu & IIM Bodh Gaya
Mode
Online (CBT), English only
Duration
2 hours 30 minutes
Attempts
One attempt per year
Difficulty
Medium

Application fee

₹2,200 (General/OBC-NCL); ₹1,100 (SC/ST/PwBD/EWS/transgender). Outside India ₹11,000

From the official 2026 notice.

Exam pattern

100 MCQs, 400 marks: Quant 33, DI and LR 33, Verbal and RC 34. +4 correct, −1 wrong

Upcoming dates

Exam20 Apr 2027

Dates move. Confirm on the official website before you plan around them. The app shows the latest dates and can remind you before registration closes.

How competitive it is

~30,000 applicants for ~250 seats — illustrative. Dates tentative unless marked confirmed — verify on the official site.

Preparation tip

Quant, Verbal and Data Interpretation; practise the section-wise time split.
