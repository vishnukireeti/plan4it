RRB NTPC

RRB NTPC is conducted by Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs). It is used for admission to Station master, clerk, goods guard, accountant.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: rrbapply.gov.in
Conducted by
Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs)
Eligibility
Class 12 for undergraduate posts (clerks, typists); a degree for graduate posts (Station Master, Goods Guard and others)
Courses
Station master, clerk, goods guard, accountant
Mode
Online CBT 1, CBT 2, typing or aptitude test (some posts), documents and medical
Duration
90 minutes per CBT
Age limit
Usually 18–30 (Class 12 posts) or 18–33/36 (graduate posts); check each CEN
Difficulty
High

Application fee

₹500 (₹400 refunded after CBT 1). Concession categories ₹250 (fully refunded after CBT 1)

From the official 2026 notice.

Exam pattern

CBT 1: 100 questions (General Awareness 40, Maths 30, Reasoning 30). CBT 2: 120 questions. One-third negative marking
