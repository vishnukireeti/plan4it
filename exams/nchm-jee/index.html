NCHM JEE

NCHM JEE is conducted by National Testing Agency (NTA) for NCHMCT. It is used for admission to B.Sc Hospitality & Hotel Administration at IHMs.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: exams.nta.nic.in
Conducted by
National Testing Agency (NTA) for NCHMCT
Eligibility
Class 12 (any stream) with English
Courses
B.Sc Hospitality & Hotel Administration at IHMs
Mode
Online (CBT), English and Hindi
Duration
2 hours
Attempts
Single attempt per year
Age limit
No age limit
Difficulty
Medium

Application fee

₹1,000 (General/OBC-NCL); ₹700 (EWS); ₹450 (SC/ST/PwD/third gender). GST and bank charges extra

From the official 2026 notice.

Exam pattern

120 MCQs: Numerical 15, Reasoning 15, GK 15, English 45, Service-sector aptitude 30. +4 correct, −1 wrong

Upcoming dates

Exam25 Apr 2027

Dates move. Confirm on the official website before you plan around them. The app shows the latest dates and can remind you before registration closes.

How competitive it is

~60,000 applicants for ~7,000 seats — illustrative. Dates tentative unless marked confirmed — verify on the official site.

Preparation tip

English, Reasoning and Service Sector Aptitude carry substantial weight.
