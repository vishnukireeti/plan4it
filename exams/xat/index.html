XAT

XAT is conducted by XLRI Jamshedpur. It is used for admission to MBA / PGDM at XLRI, XIMB, and 160+ B-schools.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: xatonline.in
Conducted by
XLRI Jamshedpur
Eligibility
Bachelor's degree with 3 years (or final-year)
Courses
MBA / PGDM at XLRI, XIMB, and 160+ B-schools
Mode
Online (CBT), single shift
Duration
3 hours (2–5 PM)
Attempts
One attempt per year
Difficulty
Very High

Application fee

₹2,300 (+₹200 for each XLRI programme)
