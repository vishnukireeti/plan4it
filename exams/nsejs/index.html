NSEJS (Junior Science Olympiad)

NSEJS (Junior Science Olympiad) is conducted by Indian Association of Physics Teachers (IAPT) with HBCSE. It is used for admission to Path to International Junior Science Olympiad.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: iapt.org.in
Conducted by
Indian Association of Physics Teachers (IAPT) with HBCSE
Eligibility
Indian students born 1 Jan 2012 – 31 Dec 2013 who haven't finished Class 10 boards before 30 Nov 2026
Courses
Path to International Junior Science Olympiad
Mode
Pen and paper, at your NSE centre
Duration
2 hours
Difficulty
High

Application fee

₹300

From the official 2026–27 notice.

Exam pattern

Part A: 48 MCQs (+3, −1). Part B: 12 multi-correct questions. 216 marks
