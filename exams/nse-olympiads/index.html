National Standard Examinations (NSEP, NSEC, NSEB, NSEA)

National Standard Examinations (NSEP, NSEC, NSEB, NSEA) is conducted by Indian Association of Physics Teachers (IAPT) with HBCSE. It is used for admission to Path to INO and international science olympiads.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: iapt.org.in
Conducted by
Indian Association of Physics Teachers (IAPT) with HBCSE
Eligibility
Indian students born 1 Jul 2007 – 30 Jun 2012 who haven't finished Class 12 boards before 30 Nov 2026
Courses
Path to INO and international science olympiads
Mode
Pen and paper, at your NSE centre
Duration
2 hours per subject
Difficulty
Very High

Application fee

₹300 per subject

From the official 2026–27 notice.

Exam pattern

Part A: 48 MCQs (+3, −1). Part B: 12 multi-correct questions (6 marks each). 216 marks
