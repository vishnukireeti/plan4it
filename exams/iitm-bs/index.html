IIT Madras BS Qualifier

IIT Madras BS Qualifier is conducted by IIT Madras. It is used for admission to BS in Data Science and Electronic Systems (online).

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: study.iitm.ac.in
Conducted by
IIT Madras
Eligibility
Class 12 passed (any stream, any age), with Maths and English in Class 10. Class 11 students can apply
Courses
BS in Data Science and Electronic Systems (online)
Mode
4 weeks of online coursework with weekly assignments, then a 4-hour in-person qualifier exam
Difficulty
Medium

Application fee

₹4,000 (General/OBC); ₹2,000 (SC/ST/PwD); ₹1,000 (SC/ST and PwD)

From the official January 2027 batch notice.

Exam pattern

Courses: English I, Maths for Data Science I, Statistics I, Computational Thinking. Pass: 40% in each and 50% average (lower for reserved categories)

Last cycle (January 2027 batch)

RegistrationOpen now; apply by 29 Jan 2027
ExamQualifier exam after 4 weeks of coursework

The next dates are not out yet. Last year's timing is a rough guide only.

Preparation tip

The qualifier is a 4-week course with an exam, so you can try it before you commit.
