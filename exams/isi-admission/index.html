ISI Admission Test

ISI Admission Test is conducted by Indian Statistical Institute. It is used for admission to B.Stat (Hons) / B.Math (Hons) at ISI Kolkata, Bengaluru, Delhi.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: admission.isical.ac.in
Conducted by
Indian Statistical Institute
Eligibility
B.Stat and B.Math: Class 12 with Maths and English
Courses
B.Stat (Hons) / B.Math (Hons) at ISI Kolkata, Bengaluru, Delhi
Mode
Offline (subjective + objective)
Duration
2 hours (+ 2 hours subjective)
Attempts
Single attempt per year
Difficulty
Very High

Application fee

₹1,500 (General men); ₹1,000 (General women); ₹750 (OBC-NCL, SC, ST, PwBD, EWS)

How competitive it is

~15,000 applicants for ~200 seats — illustrative. Dates tentative unless marked confirmed — verify on the official site.

Preparation tip

Deep problem-solving in Maths; RMO/INMO-style practice helps significantly.
