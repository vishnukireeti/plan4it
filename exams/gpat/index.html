Pharmacy · Entrance exam

GPAT

GPAT is conducted by National Board of Examinations in Medical Sciences (NBEMS) for the Pharmacy Council of India. It is used for admission to M.Pharm and AICTE scholarship eligibility.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: natboard.edu.in
Conducted by
National Board of Examinations in Medical Sciences (NBEMS) for the Pharmacy Council of India
Eligibility
B.Pharm (final-year and pre-final-year students can apply)
Courses
M.Pharm and AICTE scholarship eligibility
Mode
Online (CBT), English only
Duration
3 hours, in timed sections
Attempts
One application per year; no limit across years stated
Difficulty
High

Application fee

₹3,500 (General/OBC/EWS); ₹2,500 (SC/ST/PwD)

From the official 2026 notice.

Exam pattern

125 MCQs, 500 marks; +4 correct, −1 wrong

Upcoming dates

Exam31 Jan 2027

Dates move. Confirm on the official website before you plan around them. The app shows the latest dates and can remind you before registration closes.

How competitive it is

~60,000 applicants — illustrative. Dates tentative unless marked confirmed — verify on the official site.

Preparation tip

A GPAT score above the cutoff also unlocks the AICTE monthly PG scholarship.
