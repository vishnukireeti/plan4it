WBJEE

WBJEE is conducted by West Bengal Joint Entrance Examinations Board. It is used for admission to B.Tech / B.Pharm / B.Arch in West Bengal.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: wbjeeb.nic.in
Conducted by
West Bengal Joint Entrance Examinations Board
Eligibility
Class 12 with Physics and Maths plus Chemistry or another listed subject, 45% in these (40% reserved) and 30% in English
Courses
B.Tech / B.Pharm / B.Arch in West Bengal
Mode
Offline, OMR sheet
Duration
Two papers of 2 hours each (Maths; Physics and Chemistry)
Attempts
No attempt limit stated in the 2026 bulletin
Age limit
At least 17 years by 31 Dec of the exam year; no upper limit (25 for Marine Engineering)
Difficulty
High

Application fee

General: ₹500 (male), ₹400 (female), ₹300 (third gender). SC/ST/OBC/EWS/PwD/TFW: ₹400 / ₹300 / ₹200

From the official 2026 notice.

Exam pattern

Maths 75 MCQs (100 marks); Physics 40 and Chemistry 40 MCQs (50 marks each). Wrong answers lose ¼ of the marks in single-answer questions; no negative marking in multi-correct questions

Upcoming dates

Exam25 Apr 2027

Dates move. Confirm on the official website before you plan around them. The app shows the latest dates and can remind you before registration closes.

How competitive it is

~1 lakh applicants — illustrative. Dates tentative unless marked confirmed — verify on the official site.

Preparation tip

Category 3 questions have partial marking — practise past papers for negative-marking strategy.
