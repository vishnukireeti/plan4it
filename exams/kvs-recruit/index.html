KVS Teacher Recruitment

KVS Teacher Recruitment is conducted by Kendriya Vidyalaya Sangathan. It is used for admission to PRT, TGT, PGT in Kendriya Vidyalayas.

Cover child development, pedagogy and your subject. Previous papers show the pattern well.
