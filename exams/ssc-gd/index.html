SSC GD Constable

SSC GD Constable is conducted by Staff Selection Commission (SSC). It is used for admission to Constable in BSF, CISF, CRPF, ITBP, SSB, Assam Rifles.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: ssc.gov.in
Conducted by
Staff Selection Commission (SSC)
Eligibility
Class 10 pass, with domicile of the state chosen. Height: men 170 cm, women 157 cm (relaxed for some regions and ST). Run: men 5 km in 24 min, women 1.6 km in 8.5 min
Courses
Constable in BSF, CISF, CRPF, ITBP, SSB, Assam Rifles
Mode
Online (CBT), then physical tests and medical
Duration
60 minutes
Age limit
18 to 23 on 1 Jan of the exam year. Relaxation: SC/ST 5 years, OBC 3
Difficulty
High

Application fee

₹100; no fee for women, SC, ST and eligible ex-servicemen

From the official 2026 notice.

Exam pattern

80 MCQs, 160 marks (Reasoning, GK, Elementary Maths, English/Hindi, 20 each). −0.25 per wrong answer. Paper in English, Hindi and 13 regional languages
