AIAPGET

AIAPGET is conducted by National Testing Agency (NTA). It is used for admission to MD / MS in AYUSH streams.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: exams.nta.nic.in
Conducted by
National Testing Agency (NTA)
Eligibility
BAMS, BUMS, BSMS or BHMS with registration and internship completed by the cut-off date
Courses
MD / MS in AYUSH streams
Mode
Online (CBT)
Duration
2 hours
Attempts
Multiple attempts
Age limit
No upper age limit
Difficulty
Medium

Application fee

₹2,700 (General); ₹2,450 (EWS/OBC-NCL); ₹1,800 (SC/ST/PwBD/third gender)

From the official 2026 notice.

Exam pattern

120 MCQs, 480 marks; +4 correct, −1 wrong
