BCECE

BCECE is conducted by Bihar Combined Entrance Competitive Examination Board. It is used for admission to Polytechnic, B.Pharm, B.Sc Agriculture in Bihar.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: bceceboard.bihar.gov.in
Conducted by
Bihar Combined Entrance Competitive Examination Board
Eligibility
Class 12 (PCM/PCB); Class 10 for diploma
Courses
Polytechnic, B.Pharm, B.Sc Agriculture in Bihar
Mode
Online (CBT)
Difficulty
Medium

Preparation tip

Start with Class 11-12 maths and science. Solve past papers once the syllabus is done.
