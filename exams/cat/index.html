CAT

CAT is conducted by IIM Indore (2026). It is used for admission to MBA / PGDM at 21 IIMs and 1,300+ B-schools.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: iimcat.ac.in

IIM Indore (2026)
Eligibility
Bachelor's degree with 50% (45% reserved) or final-year
Courses
MBA / PGDM at 21 IIMs and 1,300+ B-schools
Mode
Online (CBT), three sessions
Duration
2 hours
Attempts
One attempt per year; unlimited across years

Very High
