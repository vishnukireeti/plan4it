Manipal Entrance Test (MET)

Manipal Entrance Test (MET) is conducted by Manipal Academy of Higher Education (MAHE). It is used for admission to B.Tech at MIT Manipal & Bengaluru; other UG programmes.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: manipal.edu
Conducted by
Manipal Academy of Higher Education (MAHE)
Eligibility
Class 12 (PCM/PCB) with 50–60% aggregate
Courses
B.Tech at MIT Manipal & Bengaluru; other UG programmes
Mode
Online (CBT), remote-proctored
Duration
2.5 hours
Attempts
Two attempts allowed; best score counted
Difficulty
Medium
