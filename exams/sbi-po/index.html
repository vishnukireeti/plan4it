SBI PO

SBI PO is conducted by State Bank of India. It is used for admission to Probationary Officer at SBI.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: sbi.bank.in
Conducted by
State Bank of India
Eligibility
Graduation in any discipline (final-year students can apply)
Courses
Probationary Officer at SBI
Mode
Online: prelims, mains, then psychometric test, group exercise and interview
Duration
Prelims: 1 hour (20 minutes per section)
Attempts
General/EWS 6; OBC and PwBD 9; SC/ST no limit (only the mains counts as an attempt)
Age limit
21 to 30 on 1 Apr 2026 (born 2 Apr 1996 – 1 Apr 2005), with relaxations
Difficulty
High

Application fee

₹750 (General/EWS/OBC); no fee for SC, ST and PwBD

From the official 2026–27 notice.

Exam pattern

Prelims: 100 marks (English 40, Quant 30, Reasoning 30). ¼ negative marking

Upcoming dates

Exam20 Mar 2027

Dates move. Confirm on the official website before you plan around them. The app shows the latest dates and can remind you before registration closes.

How competitive it is

~8 lakh applicants for ~600 posts — illustrative. Dates tentative unless marked confirmed — verify on the official site.

Preparation tip

Descriptive English in Mains; improve editorial reading and precis writing.
