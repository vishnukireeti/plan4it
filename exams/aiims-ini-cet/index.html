INI-CET

INI-CET is conducted by AIIMS New Delhi. It is used for admission to MD, MS, MCh, DM at AIIMS, PGIMER, JIPMER.

Mode

Difficulty

Preparation tip

Revise NCERT biology and chemistry, or your clinical subjects for PG exams. Practise mock tests.
