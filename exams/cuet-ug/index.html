CUET UG

CUET UG is conducted by National Testing Agency (NTA). It is used for admission to BA / BSc / BCom / BBA at DU, BHU, JNU, HCU, 250+ universities.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: cuet.nta.nic.in
Conducted by
National Testing Agency (NTA)
Eligibility
Class 12 (any stream) — subject requirements vary
Courses
BA / BSc / BCom / BBA at DU, BHU, JNU, HCU, 250+ universities
Mode
Online (CBT) only
Duration
60 minutes per test
Attempts
No attempt limit
Age limit
No age limit for CUET itself; universities may have their own
Difficulty
High

Application fee

Up to 3 subjects: ₹1,000 (General), ₹900 (OBC-NCL/EWS), ₹800 (SC/ST/PwBD/third gender); each extra subject ₹400 / ₹375 / ₹350. Outside India ₹4,500 (+₹1,800 per extra subject)

From the official 2026 notice.

Exam pattern

50 compulsory MCQs per test; +5 correct, −1 wrong. Up to 5 tests including languages and the General Aptitude Test

How competitive it is

~13 lakh applicants for ~2.5 lakh central-university seats — illustrative. Dates tentative unless marked confirmed — verify on the official site.

Preparation tip

Pick 3–5 subjects with the highest weight for your target course; NCERT is the entire syllabus.
