CSIR-UGC NET (Joint CSIR)

CSIR-UGC NET (Joint CSIR) is conducted by National Testing Agency (NTA) for CSIR. It is used for admission to JRF / Lectureship in Life, Chemical, Earth, Mathematical, Physical sciences.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: csirnet.nta.nic.in
Conducted by
National Testing Agency (NTA) for CSIR
Eligibility
M.Sc / BE / B.Tech / MBBS with 55% (50% reserved)
Courses
JRF / Lectureship in Life, Chemical, Earth, Mathematical, Physical sciences
Mode
Online (CBT)
Duration
3 hours
Attempts
No attempt limit; held twice a year
Age limit
JRF: up to 30 years on 1 Dec 2026 (up to 5 years' relaxation for OBC-NCL, SC, ST, PwD, third gender, women). No age limit for Assistant Professor or PhD
Difficulty
Very High

Application fee

₹1,150 (General); ₹600 (EWS/OBC-NCL); ₹325 (SC/ST/PwD/third gender). Late fee ₹500 extra

From the official December 2026 notice.

Exam pattern

Parts A, B and C, 200 marks in every subject. Negative marking in most parts (varies by subject)

Upcoming dates

Exam19, 22 and 27 Dec 2026
Registration7 Oct – 5 Nov 2026 (late fee till 9 Nov); correction 10–12 Nov
StatusOfficial

Dates move. Confirm on the official website before you plan around them. The app shows the latest dates and can remind you before registration closes.

How competitive it is

~2 lakh applicants; ~5% qualify — illustrative. Dates tentative unless marked confirmed — verify on the official site.

Preparation tip

Choose the subject where you can solve numerical problems fast; use the previous year's cutoffs.
