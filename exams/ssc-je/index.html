SSC JE

SSC JE is conducted by Staff Selection Commission (SSC). It is used for admission to Junior engineer in central govt.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: ssc.gov.in
Conducted by
Staff Selection Commission (SSC)
Eligibility
Diploma or degree in Civil, Mechanical or Electrical engineering (varies by post)
Courses
Junior engineer in central govt
Mode
Online (CBT): Paper I (qualifying), then Paper II
Duration
2 hours per paper
Age limit
Up to 30 or 32 on 1 Aug 2026, depending on the post, with relaxations
Difficulty
High

Application fee

₹100; no fee for women, SC, ST, PwBD and ex-servicemen

From the official 2026 notice.

Exam pattern

Paper I: 200 MCQs, 200 marks (Reasoning 50, General Awareness 50, engineering subject 100), −0.25 per wrong answer. Paper II: 100 questions, 300 marks, −1 per wrong answer

Upcoming dates

ExamPaper I: 2–6 Nov and 30 Nov – 1 Dec 2026
StatusOfficial

Dates move. Confirm on the official website before you plan around them. The app shows the latest dates and can remind you before registration closes.

Preparation tip

Read the full syllabus and exam pattern in the notification. Solve previous year papers.
