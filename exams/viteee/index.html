VITEEE

VITEEE is conducted by Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT). It is used for admission to B.Tech at VIT Vellore, Chennai, Bhopal, AP.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: viteee.vit.ac.in
Conducted by
Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT)
Eligibility
Class 12 with 60% in PCM (50% in Maths) or PCB for some programmes; 50% for SC/ST and J&K, Ladakh, North-East candidates. Passed in the last two years or appearing
Courses
B.Tech at VIT Vellore, Chennai, Bhopal, AP
Mode
Online (CBT), English only
Duration
2 hours (sectional time limits)
Attempts
Single attempt per year
Difficulty
Medium

Application fee

₹1,450 (Indian centres); USD 90 equivalent abroad

From the official 2027 notice.

Exam pattern

100 MCQs: Maths or Biology 40, Physics 25, Chemistry 25, Aptitude 5, English 5. +4 correct, −1 wrong

Upcoming dates

Exam27 Apr – 2 May 2027 (tentative)
RegistrationOct–Nov 2026 onward (expected)
StatusTentative

These dates are expected, not yet announced. Dates move. Confirm on the official website before you plan around them. The app shows the latest dates and can remind you before registration closes.

How competitive it is

~2 lakh applicants; rank decides campus and branch slot — illustrative. Dates tentative unless marked confirmed — verify on the official site.

Preparation tip

Class 11–12 NCERT-level questions; a rank under ~20,000 gets good branches at Vellore.
