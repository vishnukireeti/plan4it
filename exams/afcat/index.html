AFCAT

AFCAT is conducted by Indian Air Force. It is used for admission to Commissioned officer in IAF (Flying, Technical, Ground Duty).

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: afcat.cdac.in
Conducted by
Indian Air Force
Eligibility
Bachelor's degree; age 20–24 (Flying) or 20–26 (Ground)
Courses
Commissioned officer in IAF (Flying, Technical, Ground Duty)
Mode
Online written test (AFCAT), then AFSB (screening, psychological and group tests, interview; pilot aptitude test for flying), then medical
Duration
2 hours
Attempts
Held twice a year: tests in February and August, applications in June and December
Difficulty
High

Application fee

₹550 (approx)

From the official 2026–27 notice.

Upcoming dates

Exam13 Feb 2027

Dates move. Confirm on the official website before you plan around them. The app shows the latest dates and can remind you before registration closes.

How competitive it is

~2 lakh applicants for ~300 seats — illustrative. Dates tentative unless marked confirmed — verify on the official site.

Preparation tip

Verbal ability, numerical ability, reasoning and military aptitude in 100 questions.
