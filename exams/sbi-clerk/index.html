SBI Clerk (Junior Associate)

SBI Clerk (Junior Associate) is conducted by State Bank of India. It is used for admission to Clerk at SBI.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: sbi.bank.in
Conducted by
State Bank of India
Eligibility
Graduation in any discipline; must know the local language of the state you apply for (one state only)
Courses
Clerk at SBI
Mode
Online: prelims, mains, then a local language test
Duration
Prelims: 1 hour (20 minutes per section)
Age limit
20 to 28 on 1 Apr 2026 (born 2 Apr 1998 – 1 Apr 2006), with relaxations
Difficulty
High

Application fee

₹750 (General/OBC/EWS); no fee for SC, ST, PwBD and ex-servicemen

From the official 2026–27 notice.

Exam pattern

Prelims: 100 MCQs (English 30, Numerical 35, Reasoning 35). ¼ negative marking. Prelims marks don't count for the final merit

Last cycle (2026–27)

Registration11 – 31 Aug 2026
ExamPrelims September 2026; Mains November 2026
