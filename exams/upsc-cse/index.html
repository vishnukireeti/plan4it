UPSC Civil Services (CSE)

UPSC Civil Services (CSE) is conducted by Union Public Service Commission (UPSC). It is used for admission to IAS / IPS / IFS and 20+ services.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: upsc.gov.in
Conducted by
Union Public Service Commission (UPSC)
Eligibility
Bachelor's degree in any subject (final-year students can apply for prelims)
Courses
IAS / IPS / IFS and 20+ services
Mode
Prelims: objective (OMR). Mains: written. Then personality test
Duration
Prelims: two papers of 2 hours each
Attempts
General/EWS 6; OBC 9; SC/ST no limit (within the age limit)
Age limit
21 to 32 on 1 Aug of the exam year. Relaxation: SC/ST 5 years, OBC 3, PwBD 10
Difficulty
Very High

Application fee

Prelims ₹100; Mains ₹200. No fee for women, SC, ST and PwBD candidates

From the official 2026 notice.

Exam pattern

Prelims: GS Paper I (200 marks, counts for merit) and CSAT Paper II (200 marks, qualifying at 33%). One-third negative marking

Upcoming dates

Dates move. Confirm on the official website before you plan around them. The app shows the latest dates and can remind you before registration closes.

How competitive it is

~10 lakh applicants for ~1,000 vacancies (~0.1%) — illustrative. Dates tentative unless marked confirmed — verify on the official site.

Preparation tip

Read NCERTs first, then newspaper daily; answer-writing practice from month 1.
