ICAR AIEEA UG (via CUET)

ICAR AIEEA UG (via CUET) is conducted by NTA / ICAR. It is used for admission to B.Sc Agriculture / Horticulture / Forestry / Fisheries at ICAR institutes.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: icar.nta.ac.in
Conducted by
NTA / ICAR
Eligibility
Class 12 (PCB/PCM/Agri)
Courses
B.Sc Agriculture / Horticulture / Forestry / Fisheries at ICAR institutes
Mode
Online (CBT) — CUET UG based
Duration
Per CUET pattern
Attempts
Single attempt per year
Difficulty
Medium

₹800 (approx)

Preparation tip

Prepare Class 12 Physics, Chemistry, Biology/Maths with agriculture-specific GK.
