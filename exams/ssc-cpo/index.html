SSC CPO

SSC CPO is conducted by Staff Selection Commission (SSC). It is used for admission to Sub-Inspector in Delhi Police and CAPFs.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: ssc.gov.in
Conducted by
Staff Selection Commission (SSC)
Eligibility
Bachelor's degree. Men applying for Delhi Police need a car and motorcycle licence. Height: men 170 cm, women 157 cm (relaxed for hill areas and ST)
Courses
Sub-Inspector in Delhi Police and CAPFs
Mode
Online (CBT) Paper I, physical tests, Paper II, medical
Duration
Paper I: 1 hour
Age limit
20 to 25 on 1 Aug 2026 (SI Fire, CISF: 18 to 30). Relaxation: SC/ST 5 years, OBC 3
Difficulty
High

Application fee

₹100; no fee for women, SC, ST and eligible ex-servicemen

From the official 2026 notice.

Exam pattern

Paper I: 100 MCQs, 100 marks (Reasoning, GK, Quant, English, 25 each). −0.25 per wrong answer

Last cycle (2026)

Registration10 – 30 Sep 2026
ExamPaper I: 23–28 Nov 2026

The next dates are not out yet. Last year's timing is a rough guide only.

Preparation tip

Prepare for the written test and the fitness and medical standards at the same time.
