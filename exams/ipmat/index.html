IPMAT

IPMAT is conducted by IIM Indore. It is used for admission to 5-year Integrated Programme in Management (IPM).

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: iimidr.ac.in
Conducted by
IIM Indore
Eligibility
Class 12 passed in the last two years or appearing
Courses
5-year Integrated Programme in Management (IPM)
Mode
Online (CBT)
Duration
2 hours (40 minutes per section)
Attempts
One attempt per year; 2 allowed within age limit
Age limit
Born on or after 1 Aug 2006 for the 2026 intake (5 years' relaxation for SC/ST/PwD)
Difficulty
High

Application fee

₹4,130 (General/EWS/OBC-NCL); ₹2,065 (SC/ST/PwD), including GST

From the official 2026 notice.

Exam pattern

Quant MCQ, Quant short answer and Verbal MCQ sections. +4 correct; −1 wrong in MCQ sections

Upcoming dates

Exam8 May 2027

Dates move. Confirm on the official website before you plan around them. The app shows the latest dates and can remind you before registration closes.

How competitive it is

~30,000 applicants for ~1,000 seats — illustrative. Dates tentative unless marked confirmed — verify on the official site.

Preparation tip

Quantitative Ability (MCQ + SA) and Verbal Ability, no negative marking for SA.
