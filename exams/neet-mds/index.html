NEET MDS

NEET MDS is conducted by National Board of Examinations in Medical Sciences (NBEMS). It is used for admission to MDS at dental colleges.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: natboard.edu.in
Conducted by
National Board of Examinations in Medical Sciences (NBEMS)
Eligibility
BDS with State Dental Council registration and internship completed by the cut-off date
Courses
MDS at dental colleges
Mode
Online (CBT), English only, one session
Duration
3 hours (Part A 75 min, Part B 105 min)
Attempts
Unlimited attempts
Difficulty
High

Application fee

₹3,500 (General/OBC/EWS); ₹2,500 (SC/ST/PwD)

From the official 2026 notice.

Exam pattern

240 MCQs; +4 correct, −1 wrong

Upcoming dates

Exam15 Mar 2027

Dates move. Confirm on the official website before you plan around them. The app shows the latest dates and can remind you before registration closes.

How competitive it is

~25,000 applicants for ~7,000 seats — illustrative. Dates tentative unless marked confirmed — verify on the official site.

Preparation tip

Strong recall in anatomy, dental materials and oral pathology.
