BITSAT

BITSAT is conducted by BITS Pilani. It is used for admission to B.E. / B.Pharm / M.Sc. at BITS Pilani, Goa, Hyderabad.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: bitsadmission.com
Conducted by
BITS Pilani
Eligibility
Class 12 (PCM/PCB for select) with 75% aggregate and min 60% in each of P, C, M/B
Courses
B.E. / B.Pharm / M.Sc. at BITS Pilani, Goa, Hyderabad
Mode
Online (CBT), two sessions (May & Jun)
Duration
3 hours
Attempts
Up to 2 sessions; best score counted
Difficulty
Very High

Application fee

₹3,400 (male) / ₹2,900 (female) per session (approx)

Preparation tip

Speed and accuracy matter: BITSAT has 130 questions in 180 min with an English & Logical Reasoning section, so practise timed full mocks.
