SSC Stenographer

SSC Stenographer is conducted by Staff Selection Commission (SSC). It is used for admission to Stenographer in central govt.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: ssc.gov.in
Conducted by
Staff Selection Commission (SSC)
Eligibility
Class 12 pass, with stenography skill
Courses
Stenographer in central govt
Mode
Online (CBT), then a stenography skill test
Duration
CBT: 2 hours (sectional timers)
Age limit
Grade C: 18–30; Grade D: 18–27 (on 1 Aug 2026), with relaxations
Difficulty
Medium

Application fee

₹100; no fee for women, SC, ST, PwBD and eligible ex-servicemen

From the official 2026 notice.

Exam pattern

200 MCQs, 200 marks (Reasoning 50, General Awareness 50, English 100). −0.25 per wrong answer. Skill test: dictation at 100 wpm (Grade C) or 80 wpm (Grade D)

Last cycle (2026)

Registration24 Apr – 15 May 2026
Exam9 – 15 Sep 2026
