UPSC ESE (Engineering Services)

UPSC ESE (Engineering Services) is conducted by Union Public Service Commission (UPSC). It is used for admission to Group A engineering services (Railways, CPWD, etc.).

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: upsc.gov.in
Conducted by
Union Public Service Commission (UPSC)
Eligibility
Engineering degree (or equivalent listed in the notice); final-year students can apply
Courses
Group A engineering services (Railways, CPWD, etc.)
Mode
Prelims: objective (OMR). Mains: written. Then personality test
Duration
Prelims: Paper I 2 hours, Paper II 3 hours
Attempts
6 attempts (Gen); 9 (OBC); unlimited (SC/ST)
Age limit
21 to 30 on 1 Jan 2027 (born 2 Jan 1997 – 1 Jan 2006). Relaxation: SC/ST 5 years, OBC 3, PwBD 10
Difficulty
Very High

Application fee

₹200; no fee for women, SC, ST and PwBD candidates

From the official 2027 notice.

Exam pattern

Prelims 500 marks: Paper I General Studies and Engineering Aptitude (200), Paper II engineering discipline (300). One-third negative marking

Upcoming dates

ExamPrelims 31 Jan 2027; Mains 18 Jun 2027
Registration16 Sep – 6 Oct 2026 (closed)
StatusOfficial

Dates move. Confirm on the official website before you plan around them. The app shows the latest dates and can remind you before registration closes.

How competitive it is

~2 lakh applicants for ~500 posts — illustrative. Dates tentative unless marked confirmed — verify on the official site.

Preparation tip

Prelims (GS + technical) is the filter; Mains is descriptive core-engineering.
