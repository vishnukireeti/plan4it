RRB ALP and Technician

RRB ALP and Technician is conducted by Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs). It is used for admission to Assistant loco pilot, technician.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: rrbapply.gov.in
Conducted by
Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs)
Eligibility
Class 10 plus ITI in a listed trade, or apprenticeship, or a diploma/degree in Mechanical, Electrical, Electronics or Automobile engineering
Courses
Assistant loco pilot, technician
Mode
Online CBT 1, CBT 2, then a computer-based aptitude test (CBAT)
Duration
CBT 1: 60 minutes. CBT 2: 2 hours 30 minutes
Age limit
18 to 30 on 1 Jul 2026. Relaxation: SC/ST 5 years, OBC 3
Difficulty
High

Application fee

₹500 (₹400 refunded after CBT 1). SC, ST, ex-servicemen, women, transgender, minority and EBC: ₹250 (fully refunded after CBT 1)

From the official 2026 (CEN 01/2026) notice.

Exam pattern

CBT 1: 75 questions (Maths, Mental Ability, General Science). CBT 2 Part A: 100 questions; Part B: 75 trade questions (qualifying at 35%). One-third negative marking. Final merit: CBT 2 Part A and CBAT, 50:50

Last cycle (2026 (CEN 01/2026))

Registration15 May – 14 Jun 2026
