NIFT Entrance (GAT & CAT)

NIFT Entrance (GAT & CAT) is conducted by National Testing Agency (NTA) for NIFT. It is used for admission to B.Des / B.FTech / M.Des / M.FM at 19 NIFT campuses.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: nift.ac.in
Conducted by
National Testing Agency (NTA) for NIFT
Eligibility
B.Des: Class 12 any stream. B.F.Tech: Class 12 with Maths
Courses
B.Des / B.FTech / M.Des / M.FM at 19 NIFT campuses
Mode
GAT on computer; CAT (creative test) on paper
Duration
B.Des: GAT 2 hours + CAT 3 hours. B.F.Tech: GAT 3 hours
Attempts
Single attempt per year
Age limit
B.Des and B.F.Tech: under 24 on 1 Aug of the admission year (5 years' relaxation for SC/ST/PwBD). No limit for master's
Difficulty
High

Application fee

One programme: ₹2,000 (₹500 for SC/ST/PwBD). Two programmes: ₹3,000 (₹750). NRI/foreign: ₹11,100. Late fee ₹5,000 extra

From the official 2027 notice.

Upcoming dates

Exam10 Jan 2027
Registration16 Oct – 16 Nov 2026 (late fee till 21 Nov)
StatusOfficial

Dates move. Confirm on the official website before you plan around them. The app shows the latest dates and can remind you before registration closes.

How competitive it is

~35,000 applicants for ~4,700 seats — illustrative. Dates tentative unless marked confirmed — verify on the official site.

Preparation tip

Creative Ability Test is sketching-heavy; GAT tests quant, communication and GK.
