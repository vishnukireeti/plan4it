GUJCET

GUJCET is conducted by Gujarat Secondary and Higher Secondary Education Board (GSEB). It is used for admission to B.Tech / B.Pharm in Gujarat (ACPC).

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: gujcet.gseb.org
Conducted by
Gujarat Secondary and Higher Secondary Education Board (GSEB)
Eligibility
Class 12 Science from GSEB, CBSE or CISCE through a recognised Gujarat school (Group A, B or AB)
Courses
B.Tech / B.Pharm in Gujarat (ACPC)
Mode
Pen and paper (OMR)
Duration
Physics and Chemistry 2 hours; Biology 1 hour; Maths 1 hour
Attempts
Single attempt per year
Difficulty
Medium

Application fee

₹350

From the official 2026 notice.

Exam pattern

40 MCQs each in Physics, Chemistry, Biology and Maths. +1 correct, −0.25 wrong

Upcoming dates

Exam31 Mar 2027

Dates move. Confirm on the official website before you plan around them. The app shows the latest dates and can remind you before registration closes.

How competitive it is

~1.2 lakh applicants — illustrative. Dates tentative unless marked confirmed — verify on the official site.

Preparation tip

Board-syllabus-focused; Class 12 board marks are combined with GUJCET score.
