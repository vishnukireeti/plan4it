AISSEE (Sainik School Entrance)

AISSEE (Sainik School Entrance) is conducted by National Testing Agency (NTA) for the Sainik Schools Society. It is used for admission to Admission to Sainik Schools (feeds NDA).

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: aissee.nta.nic.in
Conducted by
National Testing Agency (NTA) for the Sainik Schools Society
Eligibility
Class 5 (for Class 6), Class 8 (for Class 9)
Courses
Admission to Sainik Schools (feeds NDA)
Mode
Pen and paper (OMR)
Duration
Class 6: 150 minutes. Class 9: 180 minutes
Age limit
Class 6: 10 to 12 years on 31 March of the admission year. Class 9: 13 to 15 years
Difficulty
High

Application fee

₹850 (General, defence wards, ex-servicemen, OBC-NCL); ₹700 (SC/ST)

From the official 2026 notice.

Exam pattern

Class 6: 125 MCQs, 300 marks (Language, Maths, Intelligence, GK). Class 9: 150 MCQs, 400 marks (Maths, Intelligence, English, Science, Social Science). No negative marking

Upcoming dates

Exam31 Jan 2027 (proposed)
StatusTentative

These dates are expected, not yet announced. Dates move. Confirm on the official website before you plan around them. The app shows the latest dates and can remind you before registration closes.

Preparation tip

Prepare for the written test and the fitness and medical standards at the same time.
