MHT CET

MHT CET is conducted by State CET Cell, Maharashtra. It is used for admission to B.E. / B.Tech / B.Pharm in Maharashtra colleges.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: cetcell.mahacet.org
Conducted by
State CET Cell, Maharashtra
Eligibility
Passed or appearing Class 12 (HSC) or equivalent
Courses
B.E. / B.Tech / B.Pharm in Maharashtra colleges
Mode
Online (CBT)
Duration
3 hours (Physics and Chemistry 90 min, then Maths or Biology 90 min)
Attempts
Two attempts each year; the better percentile counts
Age limit
No age limit
Difficulty
High

Application fee

General and outside Maharashtra: ₹1,300 for one attempt (₹2,600 for both). Maharashtra reserved categories and PwD: ₹1,000 (₹2,000 for both)

From the official 2026 notice.

Exam pattern

PCM: 150 questions (Maths 2 marks each, Physics and Chemistry 1 mark). PCB: 200 questions, 1 mark each. No negative marking

Upcoming dates

Exam9 Apr 2027

Dates move. Confirm on the official website before you plan around them. The app shows the latest dates and can remind you before registration closes.

How competitive it is

~4 lakh applicants for ~1.6 lakh seats — illustrative. Dates tentative unless marked confirmed — verify on the official site.

Preparation tip

Class 11 & 12 Maharashtra State Board syllabus (50:50 weightage); percentile-based rank.
