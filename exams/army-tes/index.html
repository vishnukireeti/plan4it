Army Technical Entry Scheme (10+2 TES)

Army Technical Entry Scheme (10+2 TES) is conducted by Indian Army. It is used for admission to Officer in Indian Army with B.Tech.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: joinindianarmy.nic.in
Conducted by
Indian Army
Eligibility
Class 12 with Physics, Chemistry and Maths; a JEE Main score is required
Courses
Officer in Indian Army with B.Tech
Mode
JEE Main score plus SSB
Difficulty
Very High

Preparation tip

Prepare for the written test and the fitness and medical standards at the same time.
