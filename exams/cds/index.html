CDS

CDS is conducted by Union Public Service Commission (UPSC). It is used for admission to Officer entry at IMA, INA, AFA, OTA.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: upsc.gov.in
Conducted by
Union Public Service Commission (UPSC)
Eligibility
IMA and OTA: any degree. INA: engineering degree or B.Sc with Physics and Maths. AFA: degree with Physics and Maths at Class 12, or B.E. Final-year students can apply
Courses
Officer entry at IMA, INA, AFA, OTA
Mode
Written (objective, OMR), then SSB interview
Duration
2 hours per paper
Attempts
Held twice a year; no attempt limit within the age band
Age limit
IMA and INA 19–24; AFA 20–24 (26 with a commercial pilot licence); OTA 19–25
Difficulty
High

Application fee

₹200; no fee for women and SC/ST candidates

From the official 2026 (II) notice.

Exam pattern

IMA, INA, AFA: English, GK and Elementary Maths, 100 marks each (300). OTA: English and GK only (200). One-third negative marking

Preparation tip

English, GK and Elementary Maths: 3 papers of 100 marks each; sectional cut-offs apply.
