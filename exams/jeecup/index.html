UP Polytechnic (JEECUP)

UP Polytechnic (JEECUP) is conducted by Joint Entrance Examination Council, Uttar Pradesh. It is used for admission to Diploma in engineering, pharmacy and more in UP.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: jeecup.admissions.nic.in
Conducted by
Joint Entrance Examination Council, Uttar Pradesh
Eligibility
Class 10 (diploma after 10); Class 12 for some
Courses
Diploma in engineering, pharmacy and more in UP
Mode
Offline / CBT
Difficulty
Medium

Preparation tip

Class 10 maths and science are the base.
