OJEE

OJEE is conducted by Odisha Joint Entrance Examination Board. It is used for admission to B.Tech, B.Pharm, MBA, MCA in Odisha.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: ojee.nic.in
Conducted by
Odisha Joint Entrance Examination Board
Eligibility
Class 12 (PCM/PCB) for B.Tech/B.Pharm; graduation for MBA/MCA
Courses
B.Tech, B.Pharm, MBA, MCA in Odisha
Mode
Online (CBT), English
Duration
2 hours for most papers (1 hour for some)
Attempts
No limit stated in the 2026 brochure
Difficulty
Medium

₹1,000 for one course; ₹500 for each extra course
