Maharashtra Polytechnic Admission (CAP)

Maharashtra Polytechnic Admission (CAP) is conducted by DTE Maharashtra. It is used for admission to Polytechnic diploma in Maharashtra.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: dte.maharashtra.gov.in
Conducted by
DTE Maharashtra
Eligibility
Class 10
Courses
Polytechnic diploma in Maharashtra
Mode
Online centralised admission (marks-based)
Difficulty
Medium

Preparation tip

Admission is mostly based on Class 10 marks, so there is no separate paper.
