NEET UG

NEET UG is conducted by National Testing Agency (NTA). It is used for admission to MBBS / BDS / BAMS / BHMS / BSc Nursing.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: neet.nta.nic.in
Conducted by
National Testing Agency (NTA)
Eligibility
Class 12 (PCB) with 50% (40% reserved); age 17+
Courses
MBBS / BDS / BAMS / BHMS / BSc Nursing
Mode
Pen and paper (OMR), single shift
Duration
3 hours (2–5 PM)
Attempts
No attempt limit stated in the 2026 bulletin
Age limit
At least 17 by 31 Dec of the exam year; no upper limit
Difficulty
Very High

Application fee

₹1,700 (General); ₹1,600 (EWS/OBC-NCL); ₹1,000 (SC/ST/PwBD/third gender); ₹9,500 outside India. GST and bank charges extra

From the official 2026 notice.

Exam pattern

180 compulsory MCQs, 720 marks: Physics 45, Chemistry 45, Biology 90. +4 correct, −1 wrong

These dates are expected, not yet announced. Dates move. Confirm on the official website before you plan around them. The app shows the latest dates and can remind you before registration closes.

How competitive it is

~22 lakh applicants for ~1.2 lakh MBBS seats (~5%) — illustrative. Dates tentative unless marked confirmed — verify on the official site.

Preparation tip

Biology is 50% of marks: master NCERT line-by-line and solve 10+ years of past papers.
