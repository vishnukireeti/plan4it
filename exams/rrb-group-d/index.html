RRB Group D

RRB Group D is conducted by Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs). It is used for admission to Track maintainer, helper, pointsman.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: rrbapply.gov.in
Conducted by
Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs)
Eligibility
Class 10 or ITI (post-wise details in the notification)
Courses
Track maintainer, helper, pointsman
Mode
Online CBT, then physical efficiency test, documents and medical
Duration
90 minutes
Age limit
18 to 33 on 1 Jan 2026. Relaxation: SC/ST 5 years, OBC 3, PwBD 10 to 15
Difficulty
Medium

Application fee

₹500 (₹400 refunded after the CBT). SC, ST, ex-servicemen, PwBD, women, transgender, minority and EBC: ₹250 (fully refunded after the CBT)

From the official 2026 (CEN 09/2025) notice.

Exam pattern

100 questions: General Science 25, Maths 25, Reasoning 30, General Awareness 20. One-third negative marking. PET: men carry 35 kg for 100 m and run 1,000 m in 4 min 15 s; women 20 kg and 1,000 m in 5 min 40 s

Last cycle (2026 (CEN 09/2025))

Registration31 Jan – 2 Mar 2026
