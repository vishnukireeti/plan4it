BARC OCES / DGFS

BARC OCES / DGFS is conducted by Bhabha Atomic Research Centre (BARC), Department of Atomic Energy. It is used for admission to Training school for scientist in BARC.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: barc.gov.in
Conducted by
Bhabha Atomic Research Centre (BARC), Department of Atomic Energy
Eligibility
B.E./B.Tech in listed branches or M.Sc in listed sciences, with 60% (or 6.0 CGPA)
Courses
Training school for scientist in BARC
Mode
Online test (100 MCQs, 2 hours, +3/−1) or a valid GATE score, then interview
Age limit
Up to 26 (OBC 29, SC/ST 31) on 1 Aug of the year
Difficulty
Very High

Application fee

₹500 for General/OBC men; no fee for women, transgender, SC/ST and PwD
