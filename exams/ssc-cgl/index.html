SSC CGL

SSC CGL is conducted by Staff Selection Commission (SSC). It is used for admission to Group B & C posts in central government.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: ssc.gov.in
Conducted by
Staff Selection Commission (SSC)
Eligibility
Bachelor's degree (final-year students who finish by 1 Aug can apply). Some posts need Maths or Statistics
Courses
Group B & C posts in central government
Mode
Online (CBT): Tier I, then Tier II
Duration
Tier I: 1 hour (15-minute timer per section)
Attempts
Unlimited (within age)
Age limit
18–27 or 18–30 on 1 Aug (depends on the post; 32 for Junior Statistical Officer), with relaxations
Difficulty
High

Application fee

₹100; no fee for women, SC, ST, PwBD and eligible ex-servicemen

From the official 2026 notice.

Exam pattern

Tier I: 100 MCQs, 200 marks (Reasoning, General Awareness, Quant, English, 25 each). −0.5 per wrong answer

Upcoming dates

Exam9 Jun 2027

Dates move. Confirm on the official website before you plan around them. The app shows the latest dates and can remind you before registration closes.

How competitive it is

~30 lakh applicants for ~17,000 posts — illustrative. Dates tentative unless marked confirmed — verify on the official site.

Preparation tip

Speed maths, English and reasoning; Tier-1 is a pure speed test.
