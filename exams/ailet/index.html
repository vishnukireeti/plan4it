AILET

AILET is conducted by National Law University Delhi. It is used for admission to 5-year BA LLB (Hons) at NLU Delhi.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: nationallawuniversitydelhi.in
Conducted by
National Law University Delhi
Eligibility
Class 12 (any stream) with 50% (45% reserved)
Courses
5-year BA LLB (Hons) at NLU Delhi
Mode
Offline (pen & paper)
Duration
2 hours (2–4 PM)
Attempts
No cap on attempts
Difficulty
Very High

Application fee

₹3,500 (Gen) / ₹3,000 (SC/ST/PwD) (approx)

Dates move. Confirm on the official website before you plan around them. The app shows the latest dates and can remind you before registration closes.

How competitive it is

~15,000 applicants for ~110 seats — illustrative. Dates tentative unless marked confirmed — verify on the official site.

Preparation tip

150 MCQs in 90 min with negative marking — accuracy in English and Logical Reasoning is vital.
