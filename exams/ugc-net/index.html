UGC NET

UGC NET is conducted by National Testing Agency (NTA) for UGC. It is used for admission to Assistant Professor / JRF / PhD admission.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: ugcnet.nta.nic.in
Conducted by
National Testing Agency (NTA) for UGC
Eligibility
Master's degree with 55% (50% reserved)
Courses
Assistant Professor / JRF / PhD admission
Mode
Online (CBT)
Duration
3 hours for both papers together, no break
Attempts
No attempt limit; held twice a year (June and December)
Age limit
JRF: up to 30 years on 1 Dec 2026 (up to 5 years' relaxation for OBC-NCL, SC, ST, PwD, third gender, women). No age limit for Assistant Professor or PhD
Difficulty
High

Application fee

₹1,150 (General); ₹600 (EWS/OBC-NCL); ₹325 (SC/ST/PwD/third gender). Late fee ₹500 extra

From the official December 2026 notice.

Exam pattern

Paper I: 50 MCQs (100 marks), teaching and research aptitude. Paper II: 100 MCQs (200 marks) in your subject. 2 marks each, no negative marking

Upcoming dates

Exam14–18 and 21 Dec 2026
Registration8 – 28 Oct 2026 (late fee till 31 Oct); correction 2–4 Nov
StatusOfficial

Dates move. Confirm on the official website before you plan around them. The app shows the latest dates and can remind you before registration closes.

How competitive it is

~10 lakh applicants; ~6% qualify for JRF/AP — illustrative. Dates tentative unless marked confirmed — verify on the official site.

Preparation tip

Paper 1 (teaching and research aptitude) is common to all; use it to secure the safe margin.
