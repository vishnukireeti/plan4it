TS POLYCET

TS POLYCET is conducted by State Board of Technical Education and Training (SBTET), Telangana. It is used for admission to Polytechnic diploma in Telangana.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: polycet.sbtet.telangana.gov.in
Conducted by
State Board of Technical Education and Training (SBTET), Telangana
Eligibility
Passed or appearing Class 10 (SSC) or equivalent
Courses
Polytechnic diploma in Telangana
Mode
Offline
Difficulty
Medium

Application fee

₹500 (₹250 for SC/ST); late fee ₹100, tatkal ₹300
