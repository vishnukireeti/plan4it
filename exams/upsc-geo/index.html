UPSC Combined Geo-Scientist

UPSC Combined Geo-Scientist is conducted by Union Public Service Commission (UPSC). It is used for admission to Geologist, geophysicist, chemist in govt.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: upsc.gov.in
Conducted by
Union Public Service Commission (UPSC)
Eligibility
Master's degree in Geology, Geophysics, Chemistry or Hydrogeology, depending on the post
Courses
Geologist, geophysicist, chemist in govt
Mode
Prelims: objective (OMR). Mains: written. Then personality test
Duration
Prelims: two papers of 2 hours each
Age limit
21 to 32 on 1 Jan 2027 (born 2 Jan 1995 – 1 Jan 2006), with relaxations
Difficulty
High

Application fee

₹200; no fee for women, SC, ST and PwBD candidates

From the official 2027 notice.

Exam pattern

Prelims 400 marks: Paper I General Studies (100), Paper II stream paper (300). One-third negative marking

Upcoming dates

ExamPrelims 10 Jan 2027; Mains 19–20 Jun 2027
Registration2 – 22 Sep 2026 (closed)
StatusOfficial

Read the full syllabus and exam pattern in the notification. Solve previous year papers.
