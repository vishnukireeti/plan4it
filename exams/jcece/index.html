Jharkhand CECE

Jharkhand CECE is conducted by Jharkhand Combined Entrance Competitive Examination Board. It is used for admission to B.Tech, B.Pharm, B.Sc Agriculture in Jharkhand.

Open in Plan4it.coOfficial site: jceceb.jharkhand.gov.in
Conducted by
Jharkhand Combined Entrance Competitive Examination Board
Eligibility
Class 12 (PCM/PCB)
Courses
B.Tech, B.Pharm, B.Sc Agriculture in Jharkhand
Mode
Online (CBT)
Difficulty
Medium

Preparation tip

Start with Class 11-12 maths and science. Solve past papers once the syllabus is done.
