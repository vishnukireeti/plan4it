Who it suits

Tech-loving, hands-on students who like flying, photography, and outdoor work.

How to become a Drone Pilot

  1. Class 10 / Class 12 Pass
  2. DGCA-approved Remote Pilot Training
  3. Obtain Remote Pilot Certificate
  4. Build a Portfolio of Mapping or Photography Jobs
  5. Drone Operator / Surveyor Role

Skills you need

Drone PilotingIntermediate · Technical

Safe, smooth flying is the core skill.

Where to learn: DGCA-approved Remote Pilot Training

Aerial Photography / MappingBeginner · Domain

Good aerial framing and mapping make outputs useful.

Where to learn: YouTube and Field Practice (free)

DGCA Drone RulesBeginner · Domain

Legal compliance is mandatory for commercial flights.

Where to learn: DGCA Drone Portal (free)

  • Data Processing (Photogrammetry)
  • Maintenance & Troubleshooting

Useful certifications

  • DGCA Remote Pilot Certificate (RPC)
  • Drone Mapping Certificate (Pix4D / DJI)
  • Agricultural Drone Operator Training

Good things

  • Fast-growing field supported by government drone policy
  • Short training path to start working
  • Wide uses in agriculture, media, mapping, and defence

Hard things

  • Income depends on project availability
  • Regulations and airspace rules can restrict work
