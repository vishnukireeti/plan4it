Who it suits

Sports lovers with business and organisational skills who want a career off the field.

How to become a Sports Management Professional

  1. Class 12 (any stream)
  2. BBA / BA Sports Management
  3. MBA Sports Management (optional)
  4. Internship at League, Club, or Agency
  5. Sports Operations / Marketing Executive Role

Skills you need

Sports Marketing & SponsorshipIntermediate · Domain

Sponsorship funds most leagues and teams.

Where to learn: Sports Business Journals (free)

Event & League OperationsIntermediate · Domain

Running events needs detailed planning.

Where to learn: Internship with Sports Bodies

Athlete ManagementIntermediate · Soft

Agents represent and negotiate for athletes.

Where to learn: Sports Agency Internships

  • Negotiation
  • Sports Analytics

Useful certifications

  • Diploma / PG in Sports Management
  • FIFA / IOC Sports Administration Courses
  • Sports Analytics Certificate

Entrance exams for this career

  • CUET UGUniversal · National Testing Agency (NTA)
  • CATManagement · IIM Indore (2026)

Good things

  • India's sports leagues are growing
  • Blend of business and passion for sports
  • Diverse roles in marketing, operations, and agency work

Hard things

  • Competitive field with limited openings
  • Long hours during tournaments
