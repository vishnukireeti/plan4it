Filmmaker / Film Director

Conceives, directs, and produces films, series, and ads by leading writers, actors, and technical teams.

Who it suits

Passionate storytellers with vision, leadership, and persistence.

How to become a Filmmaker

  1. Class 12 (any stream)
  2. BA Film Studies or Diploma at FTII / SRFTI / Whistling Woods
  3. Make Short Films & Build a Reel
  4. Assistant Director on Productions
  5. Director / Producer Role

Skills you need

Storytelling & ScreenwritingIntermediate · Soft

A strong story is the foundation of any film.

Where to learn: Screenwriting Books and Courses

Directing & Visual LanguageIntermediate · Soft

Directors shape how a story is seen and felt.

Where to learn: Film School Coursework

Where to learn: YouTube Courses (free)

  • Editing
  • Team Leadership

Useful certifications

  • Diploma in Direction (FTII / SRFTI / Whistling Woods)
  • Film Editing Certificate
  • Screenwriting Certificate

Entrance exams for this career

  • FTII JETDesign · Film and Television Institute of India (FTII), Pune
  • SRFTI EntranceDesign · Satyajit Ray Film and Television Institute (Deemed to be University)
  • CUET UGUniversal · National Testing Agency (NTA)

Good things

  • Huge creative expression and industry buzz
  • OTT growth is expanding opportunities
  • Potential for very high earnings at the top

Hard things

  • Income is highly uncertain
  • Long apprenticeship and networking needed
