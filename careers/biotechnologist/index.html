Applies biology and technology to develop medicines, vaccines, crops, and industrial products.

Who it suits

Curious science students who enjoy lab work, genetics, and innovation.

How to become a Biotechnologist

  1. Class 12 (Physics, Chemistry, Biology)
  2. B.Sc / B.Tech Biotechnology
  3. M.Sc / M.Tech Biotechnology or PhD
  4. Research Internships in Labs or Biotech Firms
  5. Research Associate / Biotech Scientist Role

Skills you need

Molecular BiologyIntermediate · Domain

The foundation of modern biotech.

Where to learn: NPTEL Biotechnology (free)

Genetics & BioinformaticsIntermediate · Technical

Genes and data drive drug and crop discovery.

Where to learn: Coursera Bioinformatics (free audit)

Lab Techniques (PCR / Cell Culture)Intermediate · Technical

Hands-on techniques are needed in every lab.

Where to learn: College Lab Practicals

  • Research Methods
  • Data Analysis

Useful certifications

  • GATE (BT) Qualification
  • Bioinformatics Certificate
  • Good Laboratory Practice (GLP) Certificate

Entrance exams for this career

  • CUET UGUniversal · National Testing Agency (NTA)
  • GATEEngineering · IIT Madras (2027)

Good things

  • Fast-growing biotech and pharma sectors
  • Wide range from healthcare to agriculture
  • Strong research and startup options

Hard things

  • Entry-level salaries are modest before a Master's or PhD
  • Lab work can be repetitive
