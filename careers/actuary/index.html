Actuary

Uses maths and statistics to assess risk and price insurance, pensions, and financial products.

Who it suits

Exceptional maths students who like probability, data, and long-term problem solving.

How to become a Actuary

  1. Class 12 (Maths preferred)
  2. B.Sc Statistics / Maths / B.Com or Economics
  3. Clear ACET and IAI Core Exams
  4. Actuarial Internship or Insurance Role
  5. Actuarial Analyst Role

Skills you need

Probability & StatisticsAdvanced · Technical

The foundation of every actuarial exam.

Where to learn: Khan Academy Statistics (free)

Financial MathematicsAdvanced · Domain

Used to value future cash flows and liabilities.

Where to learn: IAI Study Material

Risk ModellingIntermediate · Tool

Actuaries model risk in code and spreadsheets.

Where to learn: Kaggle Learn / Microsoft Learn (free)

  • Excel & Python / R
  • Insurance Knowledge

Useful certifications

  • Institute of Actuaries of India (IAI) Membership
  • IFoA Fellowship
  • SOA Associate (ASA)

Entrance exams for this career

  • CUET UGUniversal · National Testing Agency (NTA)

Good things

  • Consistently ranks among the best jobs for pay and work-life balance
  • Global demand and recognition
  • Strong career growth with each exam cleared

Hard things

  • Exams are very tough and take years
  • Few professionals so competition to clear is high
