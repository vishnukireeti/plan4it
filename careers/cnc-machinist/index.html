CNC Machinist / Fitter

Operates and programs CNC machines to produce precise metal and plastic parts for industry.

Who it suits

Precision-minded students who enjoy machines, measurements, and manufacturing.

How to become a CNC Machinist

  1. Class 10 / Class 12 Pass
  2. ITI Machinist / Fitter or Diploma in Mechanical
  3. Apprenticeship in a Machine Shop
  4. CNC Programming Certification
  5. CNC Operator / Machinist Role

Skills you need

CNC Programming (G-code)Intermediate · Technical

Programs machines to cut exact shapes.

Where to learn: ITI / NSDC CNC Training

Blueprint ReadingBeginner · Domain

Correct reading of drawings avoids scrap.

Where to learn: ITI Coursework

Precision MeasurementIntermediate · Technical

Tight tolerances demand skilled measurement.

Where to learn: Workshop training

  • Machine Setup & Operation
  • Tooling & Materials

Useful certifications

  • NCVT CNC Operator Certificate
  • NSDC CNC Programmer Certification
  • Siemens / Fanuc CNC Training Certificate

Good things

  • Strong demand from auto, aerospace, and tooling companies
  • Skilled operators and programmers earn well
  • Pathway to tool design or production supervision

Hard things

  • Shift work in factory environments
  • Requires patience and precision
