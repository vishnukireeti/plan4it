Who it suits

Empathetic listeners who like guiding people toward the right education and career choices.

How to become a Career

  1. Class 12 (any stream)
  2. BA Psychology / Education / any degree
  3. Diploma or Certificate in Career Counselling
  4. Internship at Counselling Centre
  5. Career Counsellor Role
