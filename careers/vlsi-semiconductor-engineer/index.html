Designs and verifies the chips that power phones, computers, cars, and AI hardware.

Who it suits

Electronics students who love digital logic, physics, and fine-grained design.

How to become a VLSI

  1. Class 12 (Physics, Chemistry, Maths)
  2. B.Tech ECE followed by M.Tech VLSI (recommended)
  3. Learn Verilog, Digital Design, EDA Tools
  4. Chip Design Projects & Internships
  5. Design / Verification Engineer Role

Skills you need

Digital Design (Verilog / SystemVerilog)Intermediate · Technical

Hardware description languages are the core of chip design.

Where to learn: NPTEL VLSI Courses (free)

Semiconductor PhysicsIntermediate · Domain

Explains how transistors work at the silicon level.

Where to learn: NPTEL (free)

EDA Tools (Cadence / Synopsys)Beginner · Tool

Industry tools used for design and verification.

Where to learn: Cadence / Synopsys Academic Training

  • Computer Architecture
  • Scripting (Python / TCL)

Useful certifications

  • Cadence Learning Certificate
  • Synopsys Academic Training Certificate
  • NPTEL VLSI Design Verification Certificate

Entrance exams for this career

  • JEE Main & AdvancedEngineering · NTA / IITs
  • GATEEngineering · IIT Madras (2027)
  • BITSATEngineering · BITS Pilani

Good things

  • India is investing heavily in semiconductors
  • Very high pay for specialised skills
  • Strong global career options

Hard things

  • Usually needs an M.Tech or strong specialisation
  • Tools and design processes have a steep learning curve
