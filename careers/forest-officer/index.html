Forest Officer (IFoS / Range Forest Officer)

Protects forests, wildlife, and biodiversity while managing forest resources and enforcing environmental laws.

Who it suits

Nature lovers who enjoy outdoor work and want to lead conservation and forest management.

How to become a Forest Officer (IFoS

  1. Class 12 (Science preferred)
  2. Bachelor's Degree in Forestry / Agriculture / Science / Engineering
  3. Prepare for UPSC IFoS or State Forest Exams
  4. Prelims, Mains, Interview & Physical Tests
  5. Training at IGNFA and Forest Posting

Skills you need

Forestry & Ecology KnowledgeIntermediate · Domain

Understanding ecosystems guides good management.

Where to learn: NPTEL / Wildlife Institute of India (free resources)

Wildlife ManagementIntermediate · Domain

Conservation depends on species and habitat knowledge.

Where to learn: WII Resources (free)

Law & RegulationIntermediate · Domain

Officers enforce forest and wildlife laws.

Where to learn: Indian Forest Act / Wildlife Act (free)

  • Fitness & Fieldcraft
  • Leadership

Useful certifications

  • Indian Forest Service Training at IGNFA
  • Wildlife Management Certificate
  • GIS for Forestry Certificate

Entrance exams for this career

  • CUET UGUniversal · National Testing Agency (NTA)

Good things

  • Meaningful work in conservation and wildlife
  • Prestigious government service with benefits
  • Outdoor lifestyle and postings in forests and reserves

Hard things

  • Remote postings and field hardships
  • Wildlife conflict and law-enforcement risks
