Management Consultant

Solves complex business problems for client companies through research, analysis, and strategy recommendations.

Who it suits

Structured thinkers who enjoy puzzles, presentations, and rapid learning across industries.

How to become a Management Consultant

  1. Class 12 (any stream)
  2. B.Tech / B.Com / Economics from a good college
  3. MBA from a top B-School
  4. Case Interview Practice & Internships
  5. Consultant / Associate Role

Skills you need

Problem StructuringIntermediate · Soft

Breaking big problems into pieces is the core consulting skill.

Where to learn: Case in Point / Case Interview Practice

Excel & PowerPointAdvanced · Tool

Consultants live in models and slide decks.

Where to learn: Microsoft Learn / Excel Practice (free)

Business AnalysisIntermediate · Domain

Links data to what the client should actually do.

Where to learn: HBS Online Materials (free articles)

  • Client Communication
  • Market Research

Useful certifications

  • Certified Management Consultant (CMC)
  • Six Sigma Green Belt
  • Data Analytics Certificate

Entrance exams for this career

  • CATManagement · IIM Indore (2026)
  • XATManagement · XLRI Jamshedpur
  • GMAT Focus EditionManagement · GMAC

Good things

  • Fast learning across industries and functions
  • Excellent exit options into leadership and startups
  • High salaries at top firms

Hard things

  • Long hours and frequent travel
  • Recruitment heavily favours top B-schools
