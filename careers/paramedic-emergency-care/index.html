Paramedic / Emergency Medical Technician

Provides emergency care at accident scenes, in ambulances, and in hospital emergency rooms.

Who it suits

Calm, physically fit students who stay steady in emergencies and want to save lives.

How to become a Paramedic

  1. Class 12 (Physics, Chemistry, Biology)
  2. B.Sc Emergency Medical Technology / Diploma
  3. Ambulance and ER Internship
  4. BLS, ACLS, and Trauma Certification
  5. Paramedic Role

Skills you need

Basic & Advanced Life SupportIntermediate · Technical

Life support skills are the core of emergency care.

Where to learn: AHA BLS / ACLS Training

Trauma CareIntermediate · Domain

Fast handling of trauma improves survival.

Where to learn: PHTLS Training

Patient AssessmentIntermediate · Technical

Rapid assessment decides treatment priorities.

Where to learn: Clinical postings

  • Ambulance Operations
  • Stress Management

Useful certifications

  • BLS / ACLS Certification
  • PHTLS Certification
  • Emergency Medical Technician (EMT) Certificate

Entrance exams for this career

  • CUET UGUniversal · National Testing Agency (NTA)

Good things

  • Lives are saved through your quick actions
  • Growing demand with expanding ambulance services
  • Short path to a healthcare career

Hard things

  • Stressful, physically demanding work
  • Night shifts and exposure to trauma
