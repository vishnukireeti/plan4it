Who it suits

Creative, people-friendly students with an eye for style and detail.

How to become a Makeup Artist

  1. Class 10 / Class 12 Pass
  2. Certificate or Diploma in Beauty and Makeup
  3. Assist a Professional Artist
  4. Build a Portfolio and Social Media Presence
  5. Makeup Artist / Salon Professional Role

Skills you need

Makeup ApplicationIntermediate · Technical

Precise application changes looks dramatically.

Skin & Hair CareIntermediate · Domain

  • Hygiene & Product Knowledge
  • Client Communication

Useful certifications

  • Diploma in Cosmetology (VLCC / Lakme / Similar)
  • Professional Makeup Artistry Certificate
  • Skin & Hair Care Certificate

Entrance exams for this career

  • CUET UGUniversal · National Testing Agency (NTA)

Good things

  • Low entry cost with quick start
  • Can start freelancing or open a salon
  • Growing demand in weddings, film, and social media

Hard things

  • Income is seasonal and depends on reputation
  • Long standing hours and heavy competition
