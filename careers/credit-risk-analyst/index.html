Who it suits

Analytical students who enjoy statistics, finance, and data-driven decisions.

How to become a Credit

  1. Class 12 (Commerce/Maths)
  2. B.Com / B.Sc Statistics / Economics / B.Tech
  3. Learn Credit Analysis, SQL, Risk Modelling
  4. FRM Part I and Internship
  5. Credit / Risk Analyst Role

Skills you need

Credit AnalysisIntermediate · Domain

Decides who can safely be lent to.

Where to learn: Coursera Credit Risk Courses (free audit)

Statistics & ModellingIntermediate · Technical

Where to learn: Khan Academy Statistics (free)

Good things

  • Growing demand in banks and fintech

Hard things
