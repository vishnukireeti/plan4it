Chemical Engineer

Designs and runs processes that turn raw materials into fuels, chemicals, medicines, food, and materials.

Who it suits

Students who like chemistry, maths, and large-scale industrial processes.

How to become a Chemical Engineer

  1. Class 12 (Physics, Chemistry, Maths)
  2. B.Tech Chemical Engineering
  3. Learn Process Design and Simulation
  4. Plant or Refinery Internships
  5. Graduate Engineer Trainee Role

Skills you need

Process DesignIntermediate · Technical

Turns lab reactions into efficient plant operations.

Where to learn: NPTEL Chemical Engineering (free)

Thermodynamics & Reaction EngineeringIntermediate · Domain

The science behind reactors and energy balances.

Where to learn: NPTEL (free)

Process Simulation (Aspen)Beginner · Tool

Industry-standard software for simulating plants.

Where to learn: AspenTech Educational Resources

  • Safety & Environmental Compliance
  • Data Analysis

Useful certifications

  • Six Sigma Green Belt
  • HAZOP / Process Safety Certification
  • Aspen HYSYS Certification

Entrance exams for this career

  • JEE Main & AdvancedEngineering · NTA / IITs
  • JEE AdvancedEngineering · IITs (JEE Advanced 2026 organised by IIT Roorkee)
  • GATEEngineering · IIT Madras (2027)

Good things

  • Wide range from pharma to energy to FMCG
  • Good PSU and core-sector opportunities
  • Flexible path into data, consulting, or management

Hard things

  • Plant locations are often outside big cities
  • Core sector pay grows slowly early on
