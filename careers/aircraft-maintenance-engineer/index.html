Who it suits

Detail-oriented, mechanically minded students passionate about aircraft and safety.

How to become a Aircraft Maintenance Engineer (AME)

  1. Class 12 (Physics, Chemistry, Maths)
  2. DGCA-approved AME Course or B.Tech Aerospace
  3. Clear DGCA CAR-66 Module Exams
  4. Practical Hangar Training and Experience
  5. Licensed Aircraft Maintenance Engineer Role

Skills you need

Aircraft Systems & StructuresIntermediate · Domain

Engineers must know how each aircraft system works.

Where to learn: DGCA-approved AME Training

Maintenance Inspection & TroubleshootingIntermediate · Technical

Systematic inspection prevents failures.

Where to learn: Hangar Practical Training

DGCA Regulations (CAR-66)Intermediate · Domain

Licensing and safety rules govern all work.

Where to learn: DGCA CAR-66 Documents (free)

  • Technical Documentation
  • Attention to Detail

Useful certifications

  • DGCA AME Licence (Category B1 / B2)
  • EASA Part-66 Licence (for Europe)
  • Human Factors in Aviation Certificate

Entrance exams for this career

  • JEE Main & AdvancedEngineering · NTA / IITs
  • CUET UGUniversal · National Testing Agency (NTA)

Good things

  • Very strong demand with India's aviation and MRO growth
  • High pay with experience and licence category
  • Global career options

Hard things

  • High responsibility and strict regulation
  • Shift work including nights and weekends
