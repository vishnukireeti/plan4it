Cybersecurity Analyst

Protects systems, networks, and data from cyber attacks by monitoring threats, testing defences, and responding to incidents.

Who it suits

Detail-oriented problem solvers who enjoy puzzles, ethical hacking, and thinking like an attacker.

How to become a Cybersecurity Analyst

  1. Class 12 (Maths/Science)
  2. B.Tech/BCA/B.Sc in CS or Cybersecurity
  3. Learn Networking, Linux, Security Fundamentals
  4. CTFs, Bug Bounty & Home Labs
  5. Junior SOC Analyst Role

Skills you need

Networking & Operating SystemsIntermediate · Technical

You cannot defend what you do not understand at the network level.

Where to learn: Professor Messer (free)

Ethical Hacking & Pen-TestingIntermediate · Technical

Finds weaknesses before real attackers do.

Where to learn: TryHackMe / Hack The Box (free tier)

Security Tools (SIEM / Wireshark)Beginner · Tool

Used daily to detect and investigate suspicious activity.

Where to learn: Splunk Free Training / Wireshark Docs (free)

  • Cloud Security

Useful certifications

  • CompTIA Security+
  • CEH (Certified Ethical Hacker)
  • CISSP (after 5 years of experience)

Good things

  • Severe global talent shortage keeps demand strong
  • Clear certification ladder for growth
  • Meaningful work protecting people and businesses

Hard things

  • On-call duty and incident response can be stressful
  • Requires continuous learning as threats evolve
