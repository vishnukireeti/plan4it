Physiotherapist

Helps patients recover movement and manage pain after injury, surgery, or illness through exercise and manual therapy.

Who it suits

Active, empathetic students who like anatomy, sports, and hands-on rehabilitation.

How to become a Physiotherapist

  1. Class 12 (Physics, Chemistry, Biology)
  2. BPT (4.5 years including internship)
  3. State Registration
  4. MPT Specialisation (Sports / Neuro / Ortho)
  5. Clinic, Hospital, or Sports Team Role

Skills you need

Musculoskeletal AssessmentIntermediate · Domain

Accurate assessment guides the whole treatment plan.

Where to learn: Clinical postings during BPT

Exercise TherapyIntermediate · Technical

Prescribed exercise is the core of recovery.

Where to learn: Physiopedia (free)

Manual Therapy TechniquesIntermediate · Technical

Hands-on skill that improves mobility and reduces pain.

Where to learn: Workshops and internships

  • Sports Rehabilitation
  • Patient Motivation

Useful certifications

  • Indian Association of Physiotherapists Membership
  • Certified Sports Physiotherapist Course
  • McKenzie Method Certification

Entrance exams for this career

  • NEET UGMedical · National Testing Agency (NTA)
  • CUET UGUniversal · National Testing Agency (NTA)

Good things

  • Growing demand from sports, ageing, and lifestyle conditions
  • Option to run a private clinic or home-visit practice
  • Rewarding to see patients regain function

Hard things

  • Physically demanding across long patient sessions
  • Building a private client base takes time
