Designs and delivers training programmes to build employee skills in soft skills, technical topics, and leadership.

Who it suits

Confident communicators who enjoy teaching adults and improving workplace performance.

How to become a Corporate Trainer

  1. Class 12 (any stream)
  2. BA / BBA / B.Com or Psychology / Education
  3. MBA HR or Training Certification
  4. Assist Trainers in L&D Teams
  5. Trainer / L&D Executive Role

Skills you need

Facilitation & PresentationIntermediate · Soft

Engaging delivery keeps learners involved.

Where to learn: Toastmasters (low-cost)

Training Needs AnalysisIntermediate · Domain

Programmes must address real skill gaps.

Where to learn: ATD Resources

Content DesignIntermediate · Technical

Well-designed material improves learning outcomes.

Where to learn: Articulate / Canva Tutorials (free)

  • Coaching
  • Communication

Useful certifications

  • ATD Associate Professional in Talent Development (APTD)
  • Train the Trainer Certification
  • SHRM-CP

Entrance exams for this career

  • CUET UGUniversal · National Testing Agency (NTA)
  • CATManagement · IIM Indore (2026)
  • XATManagement · XLRI Jamshedpur

Good things

  • Companies invest steadily in upskilling
  • Freelance training and consulting options
  • Mix of teaching and business impact

Hard things

  • Travel and workshop schedules can be tiring
  • Impact must be shown through measurable results
