Who it suits

Trustworthy communicators who enjoy money, markets, and helping people reach goals.

How to become a Wealth Manager

  1. Class 12 (Commerce preferred)
  2. B.Com / BBA / Economics
  3. NISM Exams and CFP Certification
  4. Internship at Wealth Firm or Bank
  5. Relationship Manager / Financial Planner Role

Skills you need

Investment PlanningIntermediate · Domain

Matches goals to investments and time horizons.

Where to learn: Zerodha Varsity (free)

Financial Products KnowledgeIntermediate · Domain

Advisors must know mutual funds, insurance, and bonds.

Where to learn: AMFI / NISM Resources (free)

Client CommunicationIntermediate · Soft

Trust drives client decisions.

Where to learn: Client-facing internships

  • Tax Basics
  • Risk Profiling

Useful certifications

  • NISM Series V-A Mutual Fund Distributors
  • SEBI Registered Investment Advisor (RIA) Certification
  • Certified Financial Planner (CFP)

Entrance exams for this career

  • CUET UGUniversal · National Testing Agency (NTA)

Good things

  • Rising middle-class wealth is driving demand
  • Income grows with client base
  • Option to become an independent advisor

Hard things

  • Income can be variable early on
  • Building trust and clients takes time
