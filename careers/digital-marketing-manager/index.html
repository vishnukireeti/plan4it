Plans and runs online campaigns across search, social media, email, and content to grow brands and sales.

Who it suits

Creative, data-curious students who enjoy social media, storytelling, and testing what works.

How to become a Digital Marketing Manager

  1. Class 12 (any stream)
  2. BBA / B.Com / BA Mass Comm or Marketing Diploma
  3. Learn SEO, Ads, Analytics, Content
  4. Run Personal Blog or Freelance Campaigns
  5. Digital Marketing Executive Role

Skills you need

SEO & SEMIntermediate · Technical

Drives free and paid traffic to websites.

Where to learn: Google Skillshop / Moz Beginner's Guide (free)

Social Media MarketingIntermediate · Tool

Builds audience and brand across platforms.

Where to learn: Meta Blueprint (free)

Google AnalyticsIntermediate · Tool

Where to learn: Google Analytics Academy (free)

Entrance exams for this career

  • IPMATManagement · IIM Indore
  • CATManagement · IIM Indore (2026)

Good things

  • Low entry barrier with fast-growing demand
  • Freelance and agency options
  • Mix of creativity and analytics

Hard things

  • Platforms and algorithms change constantly
  • Results pressure can be high
