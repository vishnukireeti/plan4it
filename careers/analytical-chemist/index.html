Tests and analyses materials, drugs, and products in labs to ensure quality, safety, and compliance.

Who it suits

Detail-oriented chemistry lovers who enjoy precision, instruments, and lab work.

How to become a Analytical Chemist

  1. Class 12 (Physics, Chemistry, Maths/Biology)
  2. B.Sc Chemistry
  3. M.Sc Analytical or Organic Chemistry
  4. Internship at QC or R&D Lab
  5. Analytical Chemist / QC Executive Role

Skills you need

Chromatography (HPLC / GC)Intermediate · Tool

Separates and measures compounds in samples.

Where to learn: NPTEL Analytical Chemistry (free)

SpectroscopyIntermediate · Tool

Identifies chemical structures and concentrations.

Where to learn: College Lab Practicals

Quality Control & GLPIntermediate · Domain

Good laboratory practices keep results reliable.

Where to learn: GLP / GMP Training

  • Method Validation
  • Data Interpretation

Useful certifications

  • Good Laboratory Practice (GLP) Certificate
  • Waters / Agilent Instrument Training
  • ISO 17025 Awareness Certificate

Entrance exams for this career

  • CUET UGUniversal · National Testing Agency (NTA)
  • CSIR-UGC NET (Joint CSIR)Teaching · National Testing Agency (NTA) for CSIR
  • GATEEngineering · IIT Madras (2027)

Good things

  • Steady demand in pharma, food, and materials industries
  • Clear career in quality control and research
  • Strong regulatory and compliance roles

Hard things

  • Routine testing can be repetitive
  • Lab exposure to chemicals requires safety discipline
