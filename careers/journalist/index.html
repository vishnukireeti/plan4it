Who it suits

Curious, fearless communicators who enjoy writing, current affairs, and asking questions.

How to become a Journalist

  1. Class 12 (any stream)
  2. BA Journalism & Mass Communication
  3. Learn Reporting, Editing, Multimedia
  4. Internships and a Published Portfolio
  5. Reporter / Sub-Editor Role

Skills you need

Reporting & WritingIntermediate · Technical

Clear, accurate writing is the core craft.

Where to learn: Poynter News University (free)

InterviewingIntermediate · Soft

Good questions lead to good stories.

Where to learn: Newsroom internships

Video & Audio EditingBeginner · Tool

Video and podcasts now reach large audiences.

Where to learn: DaVinci Resolve / Audacity (free)

  • Fact-Checking
  • Social Media Distribution

Useful certifications

  • Google News Initiative Training
  • Data Journalism Certificate
  • IFCN Fact-Checking Certification

Entrance exams for this career

  • CUET UGUniversal · National Testing Agency (NTA)

Good things

  • Front-row view of current events
  • Wide range from reporting to video and podcasts
  • Public impact through storytelling

Hard things

  • Irregular hours and news-cycle pressure
  • Salaries are modest at the start
