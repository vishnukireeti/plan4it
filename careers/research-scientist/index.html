Research Scientist (ISRO / DRDO / CSIR)

Conducts experiments and develops new technologies in space, defence, biology, chemistry, and materials science.

Who it suits

Curious, patient students who love asking why and working on long-term challenges.

How to become a Research Scientist (ISRO

  1. Class 12 (Physics, Chemistry, Maths / Biology)
  2. B.Sc / B.Tech then M.Sc / M.Tech (IISc, IISER, IITs)
  3. Research Internships (INSPIRE and Summer Research Fellowships)
  4. GATE / CSIR NET / ISRO ICRB
  5. Scientist / Engineer Role at ISRO, DRDO, or CSIR Labs

Skills you need

Research DesignIntermediate · Technical

Where to learn: NPTEL / Coursera (free audit)

Scientific WritingIntermediate · Soft

  • Critical Thinking

Good things

  • Work on national-scale missions and breakthroughs
  • Job stability and pension benefits in government labs
  • Strong pathway to a PhD and global research

Hard things

  • Selection is highly competitive
  • Progress can be slow and results uncertain
