Civil Services Officer (IAS / IPS / IFS)

Runs district administration, policing, and foreign service functions to implement government policy and maintain public order.

Pay figures are approximate. Real pay depends on city, employer, skills and experience. Check current openings and pay on the National Career Service portal (ncs.gov.in).

Who it suits

Public-spirited, disciplined students with strong general awareness and long-term persistence.

How to become a Civil Services Officer (IAS

  1. Class 12 (any stream)
  2. Any Bachelor's Degree
  3. Start UPSC Preparation (NCERT, Current Affairs, Optional)
  4. Prelims, Mains & Interview
  5. Training at LBSNAA and First Posting

Skills you need

Current Affairs & General StudiesAdvanced · Domain

Forms the base for both Prelims and Mains.

Where to learn: PIB / The Hindu / NCERT (free)

Answer WritingAdvanced · Technical

Mains marks depend on structured, crisp answers.

Where to learn: Daily answer-writing practice

Ethics & Public AdministrationIntermediate · Domain

Ethics and governance carry a full paper in Mains.

Where to learn: 2nd ARC Reports (free)

  • Optional Subject Mastery
  • Time Management

Useful certifications

  • UPSC Civil Services Examination (Prelims, Mains, Interview)
  • LBSNAA Foundation Course (post-selection)

Entrance exams for this career

  • UPSC Civil Services (CSE)Government Jobs · Union Public Service Commission (UPSC)

Good things

  • Unmatched authority and public impact
  • Job security with housing, vehicle, and perks
  • Prestigious career with variety of postings

Hard things

  • Selection ratio is extremely low and prep is long
  • Frequent transfers and political pressure
