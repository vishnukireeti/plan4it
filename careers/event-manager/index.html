Who it suits

Organised, outgoing students who enjoy creativity, logistics, and working under pressure.

How to become a Event Manager

  1. Class 12 (any stream)
  2. BBA / BA Event Management or Diploma
  3. Learn Planning, Budgeting, Vendor Management
  4. Internship with Event Companies
  5. Event Executive / Planner Role

Skills you need

Event Planning & LogisticsIntermediate · Domain

Good planning avoids problems on event day.

Where to learn: Event Management Coursework

Vendor & Budget ManagementIntermediate · Domain

Costs must be controlled while delivering quality.

Where to learn: Internships with Event Firms

Client CommunicationIntermediate · Soft

Clients need reassurance and clear updates.

  • Crisis Management

Useful certifications

  • Certified Meeting Professional (CMP)
  • Event Management Diploma
  • Wedding Planning Certificate

Entrance exams for this career

  • CUET UGUniversal · National Testing Agency (NTA)
  • IPMATManagement · IIM Indore

Good things

  • Growing events and wedding industry
  • Dynamic work every day
  • Can start your own event company

Hard things

  • Long hours during peak seasons
  • High stress to deliver flawless events
