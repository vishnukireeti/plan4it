Who it suits

Calm, disciplined students passionate about health, mindfulness, and helping others.

How to become a Yoga

  1. Class 12 (any stream)
  2. Certificate / BA / B.Sc in Yoga
  3. YCB or RYT-200 Teacher Training
  4. Teach at Studios, Gyms, or Online
  5. Yoga Instructor / Wellness Coach Role

Skills you need

Yoga Asanas & PranayamaIntermediate · Technical

Safe, correct practice builds trust.

Where to learn: Yoga Teacher Training

Anatomy BasicsBeginner · Domain

Where to learn: Anatomy Workshops

Class SequencingIntermediate · Soft

Where to learn: Practice Teaching

  • Meditation Techniques
  • Communication

Useful certifications

  • Yoga Certification Board (YCB) Level 1 / 2
  • Yoga Alliance RYT-200
  • Diploma in Yoga (Morarji Desai / SVYASA)

Entrance exams for this career

  • CUET UGUniversal · National Testing Agency (NTA)

Good things

  • Rising global demand for wellness
  • Online teaching and international retreats
  • Flexible and low-cost start

Hard things

  • Building a client base takes time
  • Income depends on class volume and reputation
