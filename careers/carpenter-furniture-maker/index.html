Carpenter / Furniture Maker

Builds and repairs wooden furniture, doors, cabinets, and fittings for homes, offices, and construction.

Who it suits

Creative, hands-on students who enjoy working with wood and tools.

How to become a Carpenter

  1. Class 8 / Class 10 Pass
  2. ITI Carpenter Trade (1–2 years)
  3. Apprenticeship with a Carpenter or Furniture Maker
  4. NCVT Certification
  5. Carpenter / Furniture Maker Role

Skills you need

Woodworking & JoineryIntermediate · Technical

Strong joints make furniture last.

Where to learn: ITI Carpenter Trade Training

Measurement & LayoutBeginner · Technical

Accurate measuring reduces waste and rework.

Where to learn: Workshop practice

Power Tool OperationIntermediate · Tool

Modern tools speed up production.

Where to learn: OEM / Tool Brand Workshops

  • Finishing & Polishing
  • Design Basics

Useful certifications

  • NCVT Carpenter Trade Certificate
  • NSDC Furniture Fitter Certificate
  • CNC Wood Router Operator Certificate

Good things

  • Steady demand from real estate and interiors
  • Can start as a self-employed craftsperson
  • Creative outlet in design and finishing

Hard things

  • Physically demanding with dust and noise
  • Competition from factory-made furniture
