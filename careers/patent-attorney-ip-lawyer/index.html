Patent Attorney / IP Lawyer

Protects inventions, brands, and creative works by filing patents, trademarks, and copyrights and handling IP disputes.

Who it suits

Science or engineering graduates interested in law, technology, and innovation.

How to become a Patent Attorney

  1. Class 12 (Science/Maths)
  2. B.Tech / B.Sc then LLB (3 years)
  3. Qualify Indian Patent Agent Exam
  4. IP Internship at Law Firms
  5. Patent Associate / IP Lawyer Role

Skills you need

Patent DraftingAdvanced · Technical

Patent quality decides how strong protection is.

Where to learn: WIPO Academy (free courses)

IP LawIntermediate · Domain

Covers patents, trademarks, and copyrights.

Where to learn: WIPO / IPO India (free)

Technical Domain KnowledgeAdvanced · Domain

Understanding the invention is essential to protecting it.

Where to learn: B.Tech / B.Sc Coursework

  • Legal Research
  • Negotiation

Useful certifications

  • Indian Patent Agent Exam Certificate
  • WIPO Academy IP Certificate
  • LLM in IP Law

Entrance exams for this career

  • CLAT PGLaw · Consortium of National Law Universities
  • CLAT UGLaw · Consortium of National Law Universities

Good things

  • Rare combination of technical and legal skills earns well
  • Growing startup and innovation activity
  • Global career options

Hard things

  • Requires both a technical degree and legal training
  • Detailed drafting and long hours
