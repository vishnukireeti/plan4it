Railway Services Professional (RRB)

Operates and manages train services, stations, and technical systems as loco pilots, station masters, and technicians.

Who it suits

Students who want stable government jobs with structured pay and job security.

How to become a Railway Services Professional (RRB)

  1. Class 10 / Class 12 / ITI / Diploma
  2. Choose Post Category (NTPC, ALP, Technician, Group D)
  3. Prepare for RRB CBT Exams
  4. Medical and Document Verification
  5. Station Master / Loco Pilot / Technician Role
