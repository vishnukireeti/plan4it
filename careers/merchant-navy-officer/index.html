Merchant Navy Officer / Marine Engineer

Navigates and operates cargo ships and tankers, or maintains their engines and machinery, as a deck or engine officer.

Who it suits

Adventurous, disciplined students who enjoy travel and can stay away from home for months.

How to become a Merchant Navy Officer

  1. Class 12 (Physics, Chemistry, Maths)
  2. B.Sc Nautical Science / B.Tech Marine Engineering (IMU or approved colleges)
  3. Pre-Sea Training & Sea Medical Fitness
  4. Cadet Onboard Ships
  5. Third Officer / Fourth Engineer Role

Skills you need

Navigation & SeamanshipIntermediate · Technical

Safe navigation is the deck officer's core job.

Where to learn: DG Shipping Approved Courses

Marine Engineering SystemsIntermediate · Technical

Engine officers keep ships powered and running.

Where to learn: Marine Institute Training

Maritime Safety (STCW)Intermediate · Domain

STCW rules are mandatory for all seafarers.

Where to learn: DG Shipping Website (free)

  • Ship Operations
  • Teamwork & Discipline

Useful certifications

  • STCW Basic Safety Training
  • DG Shipping Certificate of Competency
  • Sea Survival Course

Entrance exams for this career

  • IMU CETEngineering · Indian Maritime University
  • JEE Main & AdvancedEngineering · NTA / IITs

Good things

  • High tax-free salaries and long paid leaves
  • Travel across the world
  • Good career growth to Captain or Chief Engineer

Hard things

  • Months away from family in isolated conditions
  • Strict medical and discipline requirements
