Radiology & Imaging Technologist

Operates X-ray, CT, MRI, and ultrasound machines to produce images doctors use for diagnosis.

Who it suits

Tech-comfortable students who like healthcare without long medical training.

How to become a Radiology & Imaging Technologist

  1. Class 12 (Physics, Chemistry, Biology)
  2. B.Sc Radiology & Imaging Technology (3 years)
  3. Hospital Internship
  4. Advanced Certification in CT / MRI
  5. Radiographer Role

Skills you need

X-ray & CT ImagingIntermediate · Technical

Correct technique gives clear diagnostic images.

Where to learn: Clinical postings during B.Sc Radiology

MRI BasicsBeginner · Technical

MRI needs strict safety and protocols.

Where to learn: Radiopaedia (free)

Radiation SafetyIntermediate · Domain

Protects both patients and technologists.

Where to learn: AERB Guidelines (free)

  • Patient Positioning
  • Image Quality Assessment

Useful certifications

  • AERB Radiation Safety Certificate
  • ARRT Certification (for US practice)
  • CT / MRI Advanced Diploma

Entrance exams for this career

  • CUET UGUniversal · National Testing Agency (NTA)

Good things

  • Short degree with good employability
  • High demand in hospitals and diagnostic chains
  • Technology-driven work

Hard things

  • Radiation exposure requires strict safety practice
  • Career growth needs additional certifications
