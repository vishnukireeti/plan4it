Fund Manager (Mutual Funds / PMS)

Decides which stocks and bonds a mutual fund or investment portfolio buys and sells, managing other people's savings.

Who it suits

People who enjoy markets, reading company results and staying calm when prices swing.

How to become a Fund Manager (Mutual Funds

  1. Class 12 (any stream, Maths helps)
  2. BCom / BBA / Economics / Engineering
  3. MBA Finance or CFA
  4. Analyst at a fund house or brokerage
  5. Fund Manager after years of proven research
