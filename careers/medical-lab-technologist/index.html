Medical Lab Technologist

Runs blood, tissue, and fluid tests that doctors rely on to diagnose and monitor illnesses.

Who it suits

Careful, science-loving students who enjoy lab work and accuracy.

How to become a Medical Lab Technologist

  1. Class 12 (Physics, Chemistry, Biology)
  2. B.Sc MLT / DMLT
  3. Lab Internship at Hospital or Diagnostic Chain
  4. Specialise in Microbiology or Pathology
  5. Lab Technologist Role

Skills you need

Clinical PathologyIntermediate · Domain

Accurate tests guide treatment decisions.

Where to learn: Lab practicals during B.Sc MLT

Haematology & BiochemistryIntermediate · Technical

Blood and chemistry analysis form most daily work.

Where to learn: Clinical lab internships

Lab InstrumentsBeginner · Tool

Analysers must be run and calibrated correctly.

Where to learn: Manufacturer training workshops

  • Quality Control
  • Sample Handling

Useful certifications

  • Paramedical Council Registration
  • NABL Quality Training
  • Phlebotomy Certification

Entrance exams for this career

  • CUET UGUniversal · National Testing Agency (NTA)

Good things

  • Steady demand from labs and hospitals
  • Short study path with quick employability
  • Behind-the-scenes role at the heart of diagnosis

Hard things

  • Repetitive tasks and exposure to samples
  • Salaries are modest early on
