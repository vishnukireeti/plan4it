Police Officer (Sub-Inspector / DSP)

Maintains law and order, investigates crimes, and protects public safety in state and central police forces.

Who it suits

Physically fit, courageous students who want to serve the public and take on responsibility.

How to become a Police Officer (Sub-Inspector

  1. Class 12 (any stream)
  2. Any Bachelor's Degree
  3. Prepare for State SI / SSC CPO / UPSC
  4. Written, Physical, and Interview Rounds
  5. Police Academy Training and Sub-Inspector Posting

Skills you need

Physical FitnessIntermediate · Technical

Physical tests are part of every recruitment.

Where to learn: Daily training routine

Law & Procedure KnowledgeIntermediate · Domain

Officers must know IPC, CrPC, and evidence law.

Where to learn: Bare Acts (free)

Investigation SkillsIntermediate · Soft

Investigations depend on careful questioning and observation.

Where to learn: Police Academy Training

  • Leadership
  • Communication

Useful certifications

  • State Police Academy Training
  • Physical Efficiency Test Clearance
  • Cyber Crime Investigation Certificate

Entrance exams for this career

  • SSC CPODefence · Staff Selection Commission (SSC)
  • UPSC Civil Services (CSE)Government Jobs · Union Public Service Commission (UPSC)

Good things

  • Respected uniformed service with authority
  • Job security and government benefits
  • Diverse work from investigation to intelligence

Hard things

  • Irregular hours and high stress
  • Physical and mental demands are very high
