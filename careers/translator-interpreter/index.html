Who it suits

Language lovers who are fluent in two or more languages and pay close attention to detail.

How to become a Translator

  1. Class 12 (any stream)
  2. BA in Foreign Language or Linguistics
  3. Language Proficiency Certification
  4. Freelance or Agency Projects
  5. Translator / Interpreter Role

Skills you need

Bilingual FluencyAdvanced · Technical

Good things

  • Remote and freelance friendly
  • Demand in global companies, courts, and media
  • Rare language skills earn premium rates

Hard things
