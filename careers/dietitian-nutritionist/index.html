Designs diet plans and gives nutrition guidance to manage health conditions, weight, and sports performance.

Who it suits

Health-conscious students who enjoy food science and helping people change habits.

How to become a Dietitian

  1. Class 12 (Science preferred)
  2. B.Sc Food & Nutrition / Dietetics
  3. Hospital Dietetic Internship
  4. M.Sc Nutrition or Certifications
  5. Clinical Dietitian Role or Private Practice

Skills you need

Clinical NutritionIntermediate · Domain

Diet is a major tool for managing chronic disease.

Where to learn: B.Sc Food & Nutrition coursework

Diet PlanningIntermediate · Technical

Personalised plans drive results.

Where to learn: Hospital internship

Metabolism & PhysiologyIntermediate · Domain

Understanding metabolism guides safe advice.

Where to learn: Khan Academy Health (free)

  • Counselling & Habit Change
  • Food Science

Useful certifications

  • Registered Dietitian (RD) Certification
  • Diabetes Educator Certificate
  • Sports Nutrition Certification

Entrance exams for this career

  • CUET UGUniversal · National Testing Agency (NTA)
  • CUET PGUniversal · National Testing Agency (NTA)

Good things

  • Rising demand due to lifestyle diseases and fitness trends
  • Option for online practice and consulting
  • Diverse paths in hospitals, sports, and food companies

Hard things

  • Many unqualified people compete in this space
  • Building a client base takes time
