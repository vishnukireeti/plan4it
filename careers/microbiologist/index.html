Microbiologist

Studies bacteria, viruses, and fungi to develop medicines, ensure food safety, and control infections.

Who it suits

Curious biology students who enjoy lab experiments and microscopic life.

How to become a Microbiologist

  1. Class 12 (Physics, Chemistry, Biology)
  2. B.Sc Microbiology
  3. M.Sc Microbiology
  4. Internship at Diagnostic, Pharma, or Research Lab
  5. Microbiologist / QC Executive Role

Skills you need

Microbial Culture TechniquesIntermediate · Technical

Growing and identifying microbes is the base of the field.

Where to learn: College Lab Practicals

Molecular DiagnosticsIntermediate · Technical

PCR and related tests power modern diagnostics.

Where to learn: NPTEL Molecular Biology (free)

Sterile Laboratory PracticesIntermediate · Domain

Contamination ruins results and can harm people.

Where to learn: Lab Safety Training

  • Immunology Basics
  • Data Analysis

Useful certifications

  • Good Laboratory Practice (GLP) Certificate
  • Clinical Microbiology Certificate
  • Biosafety Certification

Entrance exams for this career

  • CUET UGUniversal · National Testing Agency (NTA)
  • CSIR-UGC NET (Joint CSIR)Teaching · National Testing Agency (NTA) for CSIR

Good things

  • Demand from pharma, diagnostics, and food industries
  • Post-pandemic focus on infectious diseases
  • Path to research and clinical microbiology

Hard things

  • Entry-level salaries are modest
  • Lab safety risks with pathogens
