Shoots, edits, and publishes videos and short-form content for brands and online platforms.

Who it suits

Creative, tech-savvy students who enjoy storytelling on video and social media.

How to become a Video Editor

  1. Class 12 (any stream)
  2. Diploma / BA in Mass Communication or Self-Learning
  3. Learn Editing and Storytelling
  4. Publish Content Consistently
  5. Video Editor / Content Creator Role

Skills you need

Video Editing (Premiere / DaVinci)Intermediate · Tool

The core skill for turning footage into stories.

Where to learn: DaVinci Resolve Training (free)

Scripting & StorytellingIntermediate · Soft

Good stories hold audience attention.

Where to learn: YouTube Creator Academy (free)

Camera & Lighting BasicsBeginner · Technical

Good visuals lift even simple content.

Where to learn: YouTube Tutorials (free)

  • Social Media Strategy
  • Motion Graphics

Useful certifications

  • Adobe Certified Professional (Premiere Pro)
  • DaVinci Resolve Certification
  • YouTube Creator Certification

Entrance exams for this career

  • CUET UGUniversal · National Testing Agency (NTA)

Good things

  • Low barrier to entry with huge online demand
  • Multiple income streams such as brands, ads, and freelancing
  • Full creative control and global reach

Hard things

  • Algorithm changes affect reach and income
  • Burnout and inconsistent earnings
