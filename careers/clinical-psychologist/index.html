Assesses and supports mental health through therapy, counselling, and behavioural interventions in clinics, schools, and workplaces.

Who it suits

Empathetic listeners who are curious about human behaviour and emotionally steady.

How to become a Psychologist

  1. Class 12 (any stream, Psychology helpful)
  2. BA/B.Sc Psychology then MA/MSc (Clinical or Counselling)
  3. Supervised Internships & Case Practice
  4. M.Phil Clinical Psychology and RCI Licence (for clinical roles)
  5. Counsellor or Psychologist Role

Skills you need

Active ListeningIntermediate · Soft

Helps clients feel heard and safe.

Where to learn: Supervised practice during MA / MSc

Counselling Techniques (CBT)Intermediate · Technical

Evidence-based approach for anxiety, depression, and more.

Where to learn: Beck Institute Resources

Psychological AssessmentBeginner · Technical

Tests and interviews guide diagnosis and care plans.

Where to learn: Coursework and internships

  • Research & Statistics
  • Ethics & Confidentiality

Useful certifications

  • RCI Licence (Clinical Psychology)
  • CBT Practitioner Certification
  • Trauma-Informed Care Certificate

Entrance exams for this career

  • CUET UGUniversal · National Testing Agency (NTA)
  • CUET PGUniversal · National Testing Agency (NTA)

Good things

  • Rising mental health awareness is growing demand
  • Flexible options including private practice and online therapy
  • Deeply meaningful work

Hard things

  • Emotionally demanding work needs self-care
  • Clinical licensure requires further study beyond a Master's
