Doctor / Physician (MBBS)

Diagnoses and treats illnesses, guides patient care, and specialises in fields like medicine, surgery, or paediatrics.

Who it suits

Compassionate, resilient students who can handle long study years and high-pressure decisions.

How to become a Doctor

  1. Class 12 (Physics, Chemistry, Biology)
  2. NEET UG then MBBS (4.5 years)
  3. One-Year Internship & NMC Registration
  4. NEET PG for MD/MS Specialisation
  5. Consultant / Specialist Role

Skills you need

Clinical DiagnosisIntermediate · Domain

Correct diagnosis is the heart of good treatment.

Where to learn: Osmosis / NMC Curriculum Resources

Patient CommunicationIntermediate · Soft

Builds trust and helps patients follow treatment.

Where to learn: Practice in clinical postings

Anatomy & PhysiologyIntermediate · Domain

The scientific base for all clinical decisions.

Where to learn: Kenhub (free tier)

  • Critical Thinking Under Pressure
  • Medical Ethics

Useful certifications

  • NMC Registration
  • USMLE (for US practice)
  • PLAB (for UK practice)

Entrance exams for this career

  • NEET UGMedical · National Testing Agency (NTA)
  • NEET PGMedical · National Board of Examinations in Medical Sciences (NBEMS)
  • INI-CETMedical · AIIMS New Delhi

Good things

  • High social respect and job security
  • Lifelong learning with real impact on lives
  • Specialists and private practitioners earn very well

Hard things

  • Very long training path before peak earnings
  • Long, irregular hours especially during residency
