Who it suits

Idea-driven creatives who love language, visuals, and understanding what makes people act.

How to become a Advertising Creative

  1. Class 12 (any stream)
  2. BA / BFA / B.Des or Mass Communication
  3. Build an Ad Portfolio
  4. Internship at Ad Agency
  5. Junior Copywriter / Art Director Role

Skills you need

Copywriting & Concept CreationIntermediate · Soft

Good things

  • Highly creative and fast-paced industry
  • Path to Creative Director with high pay
  • Work across brands, media, and formats

Hard things
