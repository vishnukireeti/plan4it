Leads troops and operations in the Army, Navy, or Air Force while defending national security.

Who it suits

Physically fit, disciplined students with courage, leadership, and love for the nation.

How to become a Armed Forces Officer (NDA

  1. Class 12 (Physics, Chemistry, Maths for Air Force / Navy)
  2. NDA Exam & SSB Interview
  3. Training at NDA and IMA / Naval / Air Force Academy
  4. Commissioned as Lieutenant / Flying Officer
  5. Promotion Through Ranks and Staff Courses

Skills you need

Physical Fitness & EnduranceIntermediate · Technical

Selection and service both demand top fitness.

Good things

  • Respected career with strong sense of purpose
  • Free training with housing, healthcare, and pension benefits
  • Adventure and leadership from a young age

Hard things

  • Frequent postings to remote or high-risk areas
  • Time away from family and demanding fitness standards
