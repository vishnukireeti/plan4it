Automobile Mechanic / Technician

Diagnoses, services, and repairs cars, bikes, and increasingly electric vehicles at service centres and workshops.

Who it suits

Vehicle enthusiasts who enjoy tools, diagnosing faults, and working with their hands.

How to become a Automobile Mechanic

  1. Class 10 / Class 12 Pass
  2. ITI Motor Mechanic or Diploma in Automobile Engineering
  3. Apprenticeship at a Service Centre
  4. OEM or ASDC Certification
  5. Service Technician Role

Skills you need

Engine & Transmission RepairIntermediate · Technical

The core of traditional vehicle repair.

Where to learn: ITI Mechanic Motor Vehicle Training

Vehicle DiagnosticsIntermediate · Tool

Scan tools quickly find faults in modern vehicles.

Where to learn: OEM Training Programmes

EV & Battery ServicingBeginner · Technical

EVs need new safety and repair skills.

Where to learn: ASDC / Skill India Courses (free)

  • Electrical Systems
  • Customer Communication

Useful certifications

  • NCVT Mechanic Motor Vehicle Certificate
  • ASDC Automotive Service Technician Certificate
  • EV Technician Certification

Good things

  • Growing vehicle base means steady demand
  • Can open your own workshop
  • EV servicing is a new growth area

Hard things

  • Physically demanding and oily work
  • Needs continuous upskilling for EVs and electronics
