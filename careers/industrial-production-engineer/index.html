Who it suits

Efficiency-minded students who like systems thinking, data, and continuous improvement.

How to become a Industrial

  1. Class 12 (Physics, Chemistry, Maths)
  2. B.Tech Industrial / Production Engineering
  3. Learn Lean, Six Sigma, Operations Research
  4. Factory Internship
  5. Process / Quality Engineer Role

Skills you need

Lean & Six SigmaIntermediate · Domain

Removes waste and speeds up processes.

Where to learn: NPTEL Lean Manufacturing (free)

Operations ResearchIntermediate · Technical

Optimises decisions using maths models.

Where to learn: NPTEL (free)

Supply Chain BasicsBeginner · Domain

Links plants with suppliers and customers.

Where to learn: Coursera Supply Chain Courses (free audit)

  • Quality Management

Entrance exams for this career

  • CATManagement · IIM Indore (2026)

Good things

  • Applies to any industry from factories to hospitals
  • Blend of engineering and management
  • Good pathway to operations leadership

Hard things

  • Roles are often based in industrial areas
  • Requires strong people skills to drive change
