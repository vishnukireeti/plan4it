Statistician

Designs surveys and experiments and analyses data to find patterns, test hypotheses, and support decisions.

Who it suits

Maths-strong problem solvers who enjoy probability, data, and evidence-based answers.

How to become a Statistician

  1. Class 12 (Maths preferred)
  2. B.Sc / B.Stat Statistics or Mathematics (ISI, universities)
  3. M.Sc / M.Stat Statistics
  4. Internship in Analytics or Research
  5. Statistician / Data Analyst Role

Skills you need

Probability & InferenceAdvanced · Technical

The base of every statistical decision.

Where to learn: Khan Academy / MIT OpenCourseWare (free)

Regression & ModellingIntermediate · Technical

Models relationships and predictions.

Useful certifications

  • SAS Certified Statistical Business Analyst

Entrance exams for this career

Good things

  • Growing demand as every field becomes data-driven
  • Roles in government, pharma, finance, and tech
  • Strong path to data science and research

Hard things

  • Requires strong maths and continuous learning
  • Communication of results can be challenging
