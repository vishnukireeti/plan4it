Studies the atmosphere to forecast weather, warn of cyclones and floods, and model climate change.

Who it suits

Physics and maths lovers fascinated by weather, oceans, and climate.

How to become a Meteorologist

  1. Class 12 (Physics, Chemistry, Maths)
  2. B.Sc Physics / Maths or B.Tech
  3. M.Sc / M.Tech Atmospheric Science
  4. Internship at IMD or Research Institute
  5. Meteorologist / Scientist Role

Good things

  • Growing importance due to climate change
  • Government jobs at IMD and research institutes
  • Applications in aviation, agriculture, and disaster management

Hard things

  • Few employers and openings
  • Shift work during severe weather events
