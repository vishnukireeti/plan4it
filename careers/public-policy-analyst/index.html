Researches and evaluates government policies and advises on solutions for economic, social, and governance challenges.

Who it suits

Curious, analytical students interested in governance, economics, and solving national problems.

How to become a Public Policy Analyst

  1. Class 12 (any stream, Humanities / Economics preferred)
  2. BA Economics / Political Science / Public Policy
  3. MA Public Policy / Economics / MPP
  4. Internship at Think Tanks or Government
  5. Policy Analyst Role

Skills you need

Policy ResearchIntermediate · Technical

Good evidence drives good policy.

Where to learn: NPTEL / Coursera Policy Courses (free audit)

Economics & StatisticsIntermediate · Technical

Economic reasoning underpins most policy analysis.

Where to learn: Khan Academy Economics (free)

Data Analysis (Excel / R / Stata)Intermediate · Tool

Turns survey and public data into insights.

Where to learn: R for Data Science / Stata Tutorials (free)
