Hosts radio shows and podcasts, engaging audiences through voice, storytelling, and interviews.

Pay figures are approximate. Real pay depends on city, employer, skills and experience. Check current openings and pay on the National Career Service portal (ncs.gov.in).

Who it suits

Expressive, witty students with clear voice and love of conversation.

How to become a Radio Jockey

  1. Class 12 (any stream)
  2. BA Mass Communication or RJ Diploma
  3. Practice Voice and Scripting
  4. Radio Internship or Own Podcast
  5. RJ / Podcast Host Role

Skills you need

Voice Modulation & DictionIntermediate · Technical

A confident voice engages listeners.

Where to learn: Voice Workshops

Scriptwriting & StorytellingIntermediate · Soft

Strong scripts make shows memorable.

Where to learn: Radio Station Internships

Audio EditingBeginner · Tool

Clean audio builds credibility.

Where to learn: Audacity / Adobe Audition Tutorials (free)

Entrance exams for this career

  • CUET UGUniversal · National Testing Agency (NTA)

Good things

  • Growing audio and podcast industry
  • Creative freedom and personal branding
  • Path to anchoring, voice-over, and content roles

Hard things

  • Limited openings in big radio stations
  • Building an audience takes time
