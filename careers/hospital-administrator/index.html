Manages the operations, staffing, finance, and quality systems of hospitals and healthcare organisations.

Who it suits

Organised leaders who want to work in healthcare through management rather than clinical care.

How to become a Hospital

  1. Class 12 (any stream)
  2. BBA / B.Sc / BHA or any Bachelor's Degree
  3. MBA / MHA in Hospital Administration
  4. Internship at a Hospital
  5. Hospital Executive / Administrator Role

Skills you need

Healthcare OperationsIntermediate · Domain

Smooth operations keep hospitals efficient and safe.

Where to learn: MHA coursework

Hospital Finance & BillingIntermediate · Domain

Where to learn: NABH Resources (free)

Entrance exams for this career

  • CATManagement · IIM Indore (2026)
  • CMATManagement · National Testing Agency (NTA)
  • TISSNETManagement · TISS Mumbai

Good things

  • Fast-growing private hospital and diagnostic sector
  • Leadership track without medical training
  • Good pay at senior levels

Hard things

  • High-pressure environment with 24x7 operations
  • Balancing costs and patient care can be tough
