Who it suits

Outgoing, service-minded students who enjoy people, food, travel, and fast-paced work.

How to become a Hotel & Hospitality Manager

  1. Class 12 (any stream)
  2. BHM (3–4 years) from IHM or a top institute
  3. Industrial Training at 4–5 Star Hotels
  4. Management Trainee Programme
  5. Department Manager Role

Skills you need

Guest ServiceIntermediate · Soft

Great service drives ratings, loyalty, and revenue.

Where to learn: Hotel internships

Food & Beverage OperationsIntermediate · Domain

Restaurants and banquets are major revenue centres.

Where to learn: IHM training kitchens and restaurants

Front Office ManagementIntermediate · Tool

PMS software runs check-ins, bookings, and billing.

Where to learn: Opera / Front Office Software Training

Entrance exams for this career

Good things

  • Strong global and cruise-line opportunities
  • Free meals and accommodation in many roles
  • Fast growth for standout performers

Hard things

  • Long shifts including weekends and festivals
  • Entry-level salaries can be modest
