Dentist (BDS)

Diagnoses and treats problems of the teeth, gums, and mouth, from fillings and root canals to implants and orthodontics.

Who it suits

Detail-oriented students with steady hands who enjoy healthcare and running their own practice.

How to become a Dentist (BDS)

  1. Class 12 (Physics, Chemistry, Biology)
  2. NEET UG then BDS (4 years + 1 year internship)
  3. DCI Registration
  4. Assistant Dentist or NEET MDS Specialisation
  5. Own Clinic or Senior Dentist Role

Skills you need

Clinical Dental ProceduresIntermediate · Domain

Core treatments patients come in for daily.

Where to learn: College clinical postings

Manual DexterityIntermediate · Technical

Precision work in a small space needs fine motor control.

Where to learn: Simulation lab practice

Patient CommunicationBeginner · Soft

Anxious patients need reassurance and clear explanation.

Where to learn: Clinical practice with mentors

  • Radiograph Interpretation
  • Practice Management

Useful certifications

  • Dental Council of India (DCI) Registration
  • Fellowship in Implantology
  • Clear Aligner / Orthodontic Certification

Entrance exams for this career

  • NEET UGMedical · National Testing Agency (NTA)
  • NEET MDSMedical · National Board of Examinations in Medical Sciences (NBEMS)

Good things

  • Good work-life balance and control over schedule
  • Opportunity to own a private clinic
  • Specialisations like orthodontics and implants pay well

Hard things

  • Building a patient base takes time
  • High set-up cost for a private clinic
