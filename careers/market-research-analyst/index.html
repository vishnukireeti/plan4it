Who it suits

Curious, analytical students who enjoy consumer behaviour, statistics, and storytelling with data.

How to become a Market Research Analyst

  1. Class 12 (any stream)
  2. BBA / B.Com / BA Economics or Psychology / Statistics
  3. Learn Research Methods and Data Analysis
  4. Internship at Research Agency
  5. Junior Research Analyst Role

Skills you need

Survey DesignIntermediate · Technical

Good things

  • Every brand needs consumer insight
  • Path into strategy, product, and marketing
  • Blend of data and psychology

Hard things

  • Repetitive survey and reporting work
  • Insights need strong communication to influence decisions
