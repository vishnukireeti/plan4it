Electrician / Electrical Technician

Installs, repairs, and maintains electrical wiring, appliances, and equipment in homes, factories, and buildings.

Who it suits

Practical students who like tools, problem-solving, and hands-on work.

How to become a Electrician

  1. Class 10 / Class 12 Pass
  2. ITI Electrician Trade (2 years) or Diploma in Electrical Engineering
  3. Apprenticeship under Licensed Electricians
  4. NCVT Trade Certificate & Licence
  5. Electrician / Technician Role

Skills you need

Wiring & Electrical InstallationIntermediate · Technical

Correct wiring is the base of safe electrical work.

Where to learn: ITI Electrician Trade Training

Circuit ReadingIntermediate · Technical

Reading diagrams speeds up fault finding.

Where to learn: ITI / Polytechnic Coursework

Safety PracticesBeginner · Domain

Prevents shocks, fires, and accidents.

Where to learn: NSDC / Skill India Courses (free)

  • Motor & Panel Repair
  • Troubleshooting

Useful certifications

  • NCVT Electrician Trade Certificate
  • State Electrical Contractor Licence
  • PLC / Industrial Automation Certificate

Good things

  • Quick to start earning after ITI or diploma
  • Always in demand across construction and industry
  • Can become a self-employed contractor

Hard things

  • Physically demanding with safety risks
  • Starting salaries are modest
