Who it suits

Ambitious, numbers-driven students who can handle intense pressure for high rewards.

How to become a Investment Banking Analyst

  1. Class 12 (Commerce/Maths)
  2. BBA / B.Com / BMS or Economics from a top college
  3. MBA (Finance) from a top B-School
  4. Learn Financial Modeling & Valuation
  5. Analyst Role at a Bank

Skills you need

Financial ModelingAdvanced · Technical

The core deliverable in every banking role.

Where to learn: Wall Street Prep / Breaking Into Wall Street (paid)

Valuation (DCF / Comps)Advanced · Technical

Determines what a company is worth.

Where to learn: Aswath Damodaran Lectures (free)

Excel & PowerPointAdvanced · Tool

Bankers build models and decks in these daily.

Where to learn: Microsoft Learn / Excel Practice (free)
