Who it suits

Big-picture thinkers who combine curiosity about users, business sense, and strong communication.

How to become a Product Manager

  1. Class 12 (any stream)
  2. B.Tech / BBA / B.Com then MBA (optional)
  3. Learn Product Thinking, Analytics, UX Basics
  4. Side Projects, APM Programs, or Associate PM Roles
  5. Product Manager Role

Skills you need

Product StrategyIntermediate · Domain

Decides which problems are worth solving.

Where to learn: Reforge / Lenny's Newsletter (free tier)

User ResearchIntermediate · Technical

Understanding users prevents building the wrong thing.

Where to learn: Nielsen Norman Group Articles (free)

Data-Driven Decision MakingIntermediate · Technical

Metrics show what is actually working.

Where to learn: Google Analytics Academy (free)

  • Roadmapping & Prioritisation
  • Stakeholder Management

  • Pragmatic Institute Product Management Certification
  • Certified Scrum Product Owner (CSPO)
  • Product School Product Manager Certification

Entrance exams for this career

  • CATManagement · IIM Indore (2026)
  • XATManagement · XLRI Jamshedpur
  • GMAT Focus EditionManagement · GMAC

Good things

  • High-impact and well-paid leadership track
  • Blend of tech, design, and business
  • Strong path to CPO or founder roles

Hard things

  • Few true entry-level roles so most start elsewhere
  • Accountable for outcomes without direct authority
