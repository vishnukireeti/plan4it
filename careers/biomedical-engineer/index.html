Biomedical Engineer

Designs and maintains medical devices like imaging systems, prosthetics, monitors, and diagnostic equipment.

Who it suits

Students who like both engineering and healthcare and want to build technology for patients.

How to become a Biomedical Engineer

  1. Class 12 (Physics, Chemistry, Maths/Biology)
  2. B.Tech Biomedical Engineering
  3. Learn Device Design, Signal Processing, Regulations
  4. Hospital or Device Company Internships
  5. Biomedical / Clinical Engineer Role

Skills you need

Medical Device DesignIntermediate · Technical

Devices must be safe, accurate, and reliable.

Where to learn: NPTEL Biomedical Courses (free)

BiomechanicsIntermediate · Domain

Explains how forces act on the body and implants.

Where to learn: NPTEL (free)

Signal ProcessingIntermediate · Technical

Turns raw ECG and EEG data into useful information.

Where to learn: MATLAB Onramp (free)

  • Regulatory Standards (ISO 13485)
  • MATLAB / Python

Useful certifications

  • ISO 13485 Internal Auditor Certificate
  • Medical Device Regulatory Affairs Certificate
  • Clinical Engineering Certificate

Entrance exams for this career

  • JEE Main & AdvancedEngineering · NTA / IITs
  • BITSATEngineering · BITS Pilani
  • GATEEngineering · IIT Madras (2027)

Good things

  • Growing medical device and health-tech industry
  • Bridges engineering and medicine
  • Meaningful impact on patient care

Hard things

  • Fewer openings than mainstream engineering branches
  • Regulatory processes slow down product launches
