Photographer

Captures images for weddings, fashion, products, news, and documentaries using cameras and editing tools.

Who it suits

Visual storytellers with a keen eye for light, composition, and moments.

How to become a Photographer

  1. Class 12 (any stream)
  2. Diploma or BA / BFA in Photography
  3. Build a Strong Portfolio
  4. Assist a Professional Photographer
  5. Freelance or Studio Photographer Role

Skills you need

Camera & LightingIntermediate · Technical

Light is the foundation of good photography.

Where to learn: YouTube and Photography Courses (free)

Composition & StorytellingIntermediate · Soft

Strong composition makes pictures memorable.

Where to learn: Practice and Critique Groups

Photo Editing (Lightroom / Photoshop)Intermediate · Tool

Post-processing gives images a professional finish.

Where to learn: Adobe Tutorials (free)

  • Client Management
  • Portfolio Building

Useful certifications

  • Adobe Certified Professional
  • Diploma in Photography
  • Drone Photography Certificate

Entrance exams for this career

  • CUET UGUniversal · National Testing Agency (NTA)
  • NID DATDesign · National Institute of Design (NID)

Good things

  • Creative freedom with freelance flexibility
  • Wide niches from weddings to wildlife
  • Low entry cost compared with many creative fields

Hard things

  • Income is unpredictable early on
  • Equipment costs and heavy competition
