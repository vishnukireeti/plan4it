Welder / Fabricator

Joins and shapes metal parts for buildings, ships, pipelines, machinery, and vehicles.

Who it suits

Hands-on students with steady hands who enjoy building and metalwork.

How to become a Welder

  1. Class 8 / Class 10 Pass
  2. ITI Welder Trade (1–2 years)
  3. Apprenticeship in Fabrication Shop
  4. Welding Certification Tests
  5. Welder / Fabricator Role

Skills you need

Arc / MIG / TIG WeldingIntermediate · Technical

Different welding methods suit different metals and jobs.

Where to learn: ITI Welder Trade Training

Blueprint ReadingBeginner · Domain

Welders must follow drawings and tolerances.

Where to learn: Workshop training

Metal FabricationIntermediate · Technical

Precision fabrication is the core skill of the trade.

Where to learn: Apprenticeship

  • Welding Safety
  • Quality Inspection

Useful certifications

  • NCVT Welder Trade Certificate
  • AWS Certified Welder (for international jobs)
  • NDT Level I / II

Good things

  • Skilled welders earn very well especially overseas
  • Short training with strong job prospects
  • Wide range from construction to shipbuilding

Hard things

  • Exposure to heat, fumes, and safety risks
  • Physically demanding
