Company Secretary (CS)

Ensures companies follow corporate law, manages board meetings, and advises on governance and regulatory compliance.

Who it suits

Rule-oriented, organised students who like law, business, and documentation.

How to become a Company Secretary (CS)

  1. Class 12 (any stream, Commerce preferred)
  2. CSEET & CS Executive
  3. CS Professional & 15-Month Training
  4. ICSI Membership (ACS)
  5. Compliance Officer / Company Secretary Role

Skills you need

Corporate LawIntermediate · Domain

The Companies Act is the CS's main rulebook.

Where to learn: ICSI Study Material (free)

Securities Law & ComplianceIntermediate · Domain

Listed companies depend on CS expertise for SEBI rules.

Where to learn: SEBI Website (free)

Drafting & DocumentationIntermediate · Technical

Precise drafting protects the company legally.

Where to learn: ICSI Practical Training

  • Governance & Ethics
  • Communication

Useful certifications

  • ICSI Membership (ACS)
  • Certified Independent Director
  • IFRS / Corporate Governance Certificate

Entrance exams for this career

  • CS Executive Entrance Test (CSEET)Commerce · Institute of Company Secretaries of India (ICSI)
  • CS ProfessionalCommerce · Institute of Company Secretaries of India (ICSI)

Good things

  • Low course cost and strong job security
  • Mandatory role in many companies
  • Good path to compliance and legal leadership

Hard things

  • Roles are concentrated in India
  • Exam syllabus is heavy and pass rates are low
