Commercial Pilot

Flies passenger and cargo aircraft for airlines, ensuring safe navigation, takeoff, and landing.

Who it suits

Calm, focused students with good spatial awareness and love for aviation.

How to become a Commercial Pilot

  1. Class 12 (Physics, Maths required)
  2. DGCA Class 1 Medical and Ground Exams
  3. Flight Training (150–200 hours) and CPL
  4. Type Rating for Airline Aircraft
  5. First Officer Role at an Airline

Skills you need

Flight NavigationAdvanced · Technical

Precise navigation keeps flights safe and efficient.

Where to learn: Flight school ground classes

Situational AwarenessAdvanced · Soft

Pilots must track many changing factors at once.

Where to learn: Simulator practice

Aviation RegulationsIntermediate · Domain

DGCA rules govern every flight decision.

Where to learn: DGCA Website (free)

  • Crew Resource Management
  • Decision Making Under Pressure

Useful certifications

  • DGCA Commercial Pilot Licence (CPL)
  • Instrument Rating (IR)
  • Type Rating (A320 / B737)

Entrance exams for this career

  • IGRUA EntranceDefence · Indira Gandhi Rashtriya Uran Akademi

Good things

  • Strong demand as Indian aviation expands
  • High salaries and global mobility
  • Travel and unique lifestyle

Hard things

  • Very expensive training (often 35–60 lakh)
  • Irregular schedules and strict medical requirements
