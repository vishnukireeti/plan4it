Provides in-house legal advice to companies on contracts, compliance, mergers, and regulatory matters.

Who it suits

Analytical law students interested in business, deals, and corporate strategy.

How to become a Corporate Counsel

  1. Class 12 (any stream)
  2. CLAT then BA LLB / BBA LLB (5 years)
  3. Internships at Law Firms and Companies
  4. Associate at a Law Firm
  5. In-House Counsel Role

Skills you need

Contract DraftingIntermediate · Technical

Well-drafted contracts prevent future disputes.

Where to learn: Law Firm Internships

Corporate & Commercial LawIntermediate · Domain

Corporate law underpins all business transactions.

Where to learn: NLU Coursework

Compliance & RegulationIntermediate · Domain

Companies must follow many overlapping regulations.

Where to learn: SEBI / MCA Websites (free)

  • Negotiation
  • Legal Research

Useful certifications

  • AIBE (Bar Council Enrolment)
  • LLM in Corporate Law
  • Certified Compliance Professional

Entrance exams for this career

  • CLAT UGLaw · Consortium of National Law Universities
  • AILETLaw · National Law University Delhi
  • CLAT PGLaw · Consortium of National Law Universities

Good things

  • High-paying and respected corporate role
  • Better work-life balance than litigation in many companies
  • Direct involvement in business decisions

Hard things

  • Competitive entry into top firms and companies
  • Heavy documentation and deadline pressure
