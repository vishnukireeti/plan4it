Who it suits

Analytical students who like risk, detail, and applying rules to real cases.

How to become a Insurance Underwriter

  1. Class 12 (any stream)
  2. B.Com / BBA / Statistics or Economics
  3. Insurance Institute Certification
  4. Trainee at Insurer
  5. Underwriter / Claims Analyst Role

Skills you need

Risk AssessmentIntermediate · Domain

Correct risk evaluation protects insurer profits.

Where to learn: IC-38 / Insurance Institute Study Material

Insurance ProductsIntermediate · Domain

Understanding products avoids mistakes in issuing policies.

Where to learn: IRDAI Website (free)

Data AnalysisBeginner · Tool

Data helps price and detect fraudulent claims.

Where to learn: Excel and SQL Tutorials (free)

  • Regulatory Knowledge (IRDAI)
  • Decision Making

Useful certifications

  • Licentiate / Associate of Insurance Institute of India
  • CII Certificate in Insurance (UK)
  • Chartered Insurance Professional (CPCU)

Entrance exams for this career

  • CUET UGUniversal · National Testing Agency (NTA)

Good things

  • Stable industry with steady demand
  • Good work-life balance
  • Path into risk management and actuarial work

Hard things

  • Structured and rule-driven work
  • Career growth can be slow without certifications
