Plans, coordinates, and delivers projects on time and on budget across IT, construction, and other industries.

Who it suits

Organised leaders who enjoy planning, coordinating teams, and getting things done.

How to become a Project Manager

  1. Class 12 (any stream)
  2. B.Tech / BBA / B.Com
  3. Work Experience or MBA
  4. PMP or Agile Certification
  5. Project Coordinator then Project Manager Role

Skills you need

Project Planning & SchedulingIntermediate · Domain

Good plans keep projects on track.

Where to learn: PMI Resources / Coursera (free audit)

Risk ManagementIntermediate · Domain

Anticipating problems prevents costly delays.

Where to learn: PMBOK Guide

Stakeholder CommunicationIntermediate · Soft

Aligned stakeholders make delivery smoother.

Where to learn: Team Project Experience

  • Agile / Scrum
  • Budgeting

Useful certifications

  • PMP (Project Management Professional)
  • PRINCE2 Foundation
  • Certified ScrumMaster (CSM)

Good things

  • High demand across all industries
  • Globally recognised certifications like PMP
  • Direct path to senior leadership

Hard things

  • Accountable for deadlines and team issues
  • Stress from scope and budget changes
