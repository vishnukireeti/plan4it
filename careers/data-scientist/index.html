Who it suits

Analytical thinkers who enjoy math, storytelling with data, and solving open-ended business problems.

How to become a Data Scientist

  1. Class 12 (Maths/Science)
  2. B.Tech/B.Sc in CS, Statistics, or Data Science
  3. Learn Python, Statistics, ML, SQL
  4. Kaggle Competitions & End-to-End Projects
  5. Junior Data Scientist Role

Skills you need

PythonIntermediate · Technical

Used for cleaning data, modelling, and automation.

Where to learn: Kaggle Learn (free)

Statistics & ProbabilityIntermediate · Technical

Underpins every experiment and model you will evaluate.

Where to learn: Khan Academy Statistics (free)

Machine LearningIntermediate · Technical

Turns raw data into predictions the business can act on.

Where to learn: Andrew Ng ML Specialization (Coursera)
