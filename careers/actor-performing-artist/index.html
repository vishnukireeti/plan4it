Actor / Performing Artist

Performs in films, television, theatre, web series, and ads by bringing scripts and characters to life.

Pay figures are approximate. Real pay depends on city, employer, skills and experience. Check current openings and pay on the National Career Service portal (ncs.gov.in).

Who it suits

Expressive, confident students with strong communication, emotion, and stage presence.

How to become a Actor

  1. Class 12 (any stream)
  2. Diploma in Acting or BA Theatre / Drama
  3. Theatre Practice and Workshops
  4. Build a Showreel and Audition
  5. Actor / Performing Artist Role

Skills you need

Acting TechniqueIntermediate · Soft

Craft turns talent into believable performances.

Where to learn: Theatre Groups and Acting Workshops

Voice & DictionIntermediate · Technical

Clear, expressive speech is essential.

Where to learn: Voice Workshops

Movement & ExpressionIntermediate · Soft

Adaptability helps on set and on stage.

Where to learn: Improv Classes

  • Improvisation
  • Resilience

Useful certifications

  • Diploma in Acting (NSD / FTII / Film Schools)
  • Theatre Workshop Certificates
  • Voice and Speech Certificate

Entrance exams for this career

  • FTII JETDesign · Film and Television Institute of India (FTII), Pune
  • CUET UGUniversal · National Testing Agency (NTA)

Good things

  • Creative and often glamorous work
  • OTT and regional cinema are expanding roles
  • Very high upside for successful actors

Hard things

  • Extremely competitive and uncertain income
  • Rejection is frequent
