Librarian / Information Scientist

Organises, manages, and helps people find information in libraries, archives, and digital knowledge systems.

Who it suits

Quiet, organised readers who like books, research, and helping others find knowledge.

How to become a Librarian

  1. Class 12 (any stream)
  2. Bachelor's Degree then B.Lib.I.Sc
  3. M.Lib.I.Sc and UGC NET (optional)
  4. Internship at a Library
  5. Librarian / Information Officer Role

Skills you need

Useful certifications

  • Archives and Records Management Certificate

Entrance exams for this career

  • CUET UGUniversal · National Testing Agency (NTA)
  • CUET PGUniversal · National Testing Agency (NTA)
  • UGC NETTeaching · National Testing Agency (NTA) for UGC

Good things

  • Stable jobs in schools, universities, and government
  • Calm work environment with good hours
  • Growing roles in digital archives and data curation

Hard things

  • Limited number of openings
  • Salaries are modest and growth is slow
