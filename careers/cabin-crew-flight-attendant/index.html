Who it suits

Outgoing, well-groomed students who enjoy travel, service, and working in teams.

How to become a Cabin Crew

  1. Class 12 (any stream)
  2. Diploma / BBA in Aviation and Hospitality (optional)
  3. Airline Recruitment and Selection
  4. Safety Training and Certification
  5. Cabin Crew Role

Skills you need

Passenger Safety ProceduresIntermediate · Domain

Safety is the primary responsibility on board.

Where to learn: Airline Training Programme

Customer ServiceIntermediate · Soft

Warm service defines passenger experience.

Where to learn: Hospitality Internships

Communication & GroomingBeginner · Soft

Clear speech and confident presence are key.

Where to learn: Communication Workshops

  • First Aid & Emergency Response
  • Teamwork

Useful certifications

  • DGCA Cabin Crew Safety Certificate
  • IATA Cabin Crew Certificate
  • First Aid & CPR Certification

Entrance exams for this career

  • CUET UGUniversal · National Testing Agency (NTA)

Good things

  • Free or discounted air travel
  • Chance to see the world
  • Growing airline hiring in India and the Gulf

Hard things

  • Irregular schedules and jet lag
  • Physically demanding and time away from home
