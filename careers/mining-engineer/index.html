Mining Engineer

Plans, designs, and manages the safe extraction of coal, metals, and minerals from mines.

Who it suits

Practical, adventurous students who like geology, machinery, and outdoor work.

How to become a Mining Engineer

  1. Class 12 (Physics, Chemistry, Maths)
  2. B.Tech Mining Engineering
  3. Learn Mine Design, Safety, Surveying
  4. Mine Internship
  5. Junior Mining Engineer Role

Skills you need

Mine Planning & DesignIntermediate · Domain

Good planning maximises yield and safety.

Where to learn: NPTEL Mining Engineering (free)

Rock MechanicsIntermediate · Technical

Rock behaviour decides mine stability.

Where to learn: NPTEL (free)

Mine SafetyIntermediate · Domain

Safety compliance is critical in every mine.

Entrance exams for this career

Good things

  • Strong PSU jobs such as Coal India
  • Good overseas opportunities
  • Essential to steel, power, and infrastructure

Hard things

  • Remote locations and harsh working conditions
  • Safety and environmental risks
