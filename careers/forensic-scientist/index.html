Forensic Scientist

Analyses physical, chemical, and digital evidence from crime scenes to support investigations and court cases.

Who it suits

Detail-oriented science students who enjoy solving puzzles and supporting justice.

How to become a Forensic Scientist

  1. Class 12 (Physics, Chemistry, Biology)
  2. B.Sc Forensic Science (or NFSU programmes)
  3. M.Sc Forensic Science
  4. Internship at Forensic Lab
  5. Forensic Analyst / Scientist Role

Skills you need

Crime Scene AnalysisIntermediate · Domain

Proper evidence handling keeps cases valid.

Entrance exams for this career

  • CUET UGUniversal · National Testing Agency (NTA)
  • CUET PGUniversal · National Testing Agency (NTA)

Good things

  • Fascinating work supporting justice
  • Government forensic labs and private firms hire
  • Growing cyber and digital forensics demand

Hard things

  • Limited government vacancies
  • Exposure to disturbing case material
