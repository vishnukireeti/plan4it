HVAC / Refrigeration Technician

Installs and services air conditioners, chillers, and refrigeration systems in homes, offices, malls, and cold chains.

Who it suits

Practical students who like machines, cooling systems, and on-site problem solving.

How to become a HVAC

  1. Class 10 / Class 12 Pass
  2. ITI Refrigeration & AC Mechanic (2 years)
  3. Apprenticeship with a Service Company
  4. Brand or OEM Certification
  5. HVAC Technician Role

Skills you need

Useful certifications

  • NCVT Refrigeration & AC Mechanic Certificate
  • EPA Section 608 Certification (for US work)
  • BEE Energy Efficiency Training

Good things

  • Rising demand due to heat and growth of cold chains
  • Good overseas opportunities
  • Can start own service business

Hard things

  • Peak-season work pressure in summer
  • Physically demanding site work
