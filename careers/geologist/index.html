Geologist / Geoscientist

Studies Earth's rocks, minerals, water, and structures to find resources and assess natural hazards.

Who it suits

Outdoor-loving science students who enjoy fieldwork, maps, and understanding the Earth.

How to become a Geologist

  1. Class 12 (Physics, Chemistry, Maths/Biology)
  2. B.Sc Geology / Applied Geology
  3. M.Sc / M.Tech Geology or Geophysics
  4. Field Training and Internship
  5. Geologist Role (GSI, ONGC, Private)

Good things

  • Strong roles in oil, mining, and government surveys
  • Global mobility in resource industries
  • Mix of fieldwork and lab or data work

Hard things

  • Remote field postings
  • Industry linked to commodity cycles
