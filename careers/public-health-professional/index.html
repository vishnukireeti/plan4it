Public Health Professional

Plans and evaluates health programmes, studies disease patterns, and works to improve community and population health.

Who it suits

Socially motivated students who like data, policy, and large-scale impact on health.

How to become a Public Health Professional

  1. Class 12 (Science preferred)
  2. B.Sc Life Sciences / Nursing / BDS / MBBS
  3. Master of Public Health (MPH)
  4. Internships with NGOs or Health Departments
  5. Public Health Officer / Programme Manager Role

Skills you need

EpidemiologyIntermediate · Domain

Tracks how diseases spread and how to control them.

Where to learn: Johns Hopkins Coursera Courses (free audit)

BiostatisticsIntermediate · Technical

Turns health data into evidence.

Where to learn: Khan Academy Statistics (free)

Health Programme ManagementIntermediate · Domain

Programmes must be planned, funded, and evaluated.

Where to learn: WHO OpenWHO Courses (free)

  • Health Policy
  • Data Analysis (R / Excel)

Useful certifications

  • Certificate in Epidemiology
  • Health Policy & Management Certificate
  • Field Epidemiology Training Programme

Entrance exams for this career

  • CUET PGUniversal · National Testing Agency (NTA)
  • TISSNETManagement · TISS Mumbai

Good things

  • Growing demand for epidemiologists and health programme managers
  • Opportunities with WHO, UNICEF, and NGOs
  • Blend of data, policy, and fieldwork

Hard things

  • Field postings in remote areas
  • Funding-based projects can be short-term
