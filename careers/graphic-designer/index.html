Creates logos, ads, packaging, social media graphics, and brand visuals using design software.

Who it suits

Visual thinkers who love typography, colour, and turning ideas into images.

How to become a Graphic Designer

  1. Class 12 (any stream)
  2. B.Des / BFA / Diploma in Graphic Design
  3. Learn Adobe Suite and Design Fundamentals
  4. Build a Portfolio with Real Projects
  5. Junior Graphic Designer Role

Skills you need

Adobe Photoshop / IllustratorIntermediate · Tool

Industry-standard tools for professional design.

Where to learn: Adobe Tutorials (free)

Typography & LayoutIntermediate · Technical

Good typography makes designs readable and premium.

Where to learn: Google Fonts Knowledge (free)

Branding & IdentityIntermediate · Domain

Identity systems give brands a consistent look.

Where to learn: Brand Design Case Studies (free)

  • Colour Theory
  • Client Communication

Useful certifications

  • Adobe Certified Professional
  • Google UX Design Certificate
  • Diploma in Graphic Design

Entrance exams for this career

  • UCEEDDesign · IIT Bombay
  • NID DATDesign · National Institute of Design (NID)
  • CUET UGUniversal · National Testing Agency (NTA)

Good things

  • Constant demand across brands and agencies
  • Freelance and remote opportunities
  • Low equipment cost to start

Hard things

  • Client revisions and subjective feedback
  • AI tools are changing entry-level work
