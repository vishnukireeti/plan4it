Who it suits

Outgoing travel lovers who enjoy culture, planning, and customer service.

How to become a Travel & Tourism Professional

  1. Class 12 (any stream)
  2. BBA / BA Travel and Tourism
  3. Learn Travel Booking Systems
  4. Internship at Travel Agency or Tour Operator
  5. Travel Consultant / Tour Manager Role

Skills you need

Itinerary PlanningIntermediate · Domain

Good plans make trips smooth and memorable.

Where to learn: Travel Agency Internships

Customer ServiceIntermediate · Soft

Happy travellers return and recommend.

Where to learn: Hospitality Practice

Destination KnowledgeBeginner · Domain

Knowledge of places lifts sales and service.

Where to learn: Tourism Board Websites (free)

  • Booking Systems (GDS)
  • Sales & Marketing

Useful certifications

  • IATA Travel and Tourism Foundation Certificate
  • Amadeus / Sabre GDS Certificate
  • Tour Guide Licence (Ministry of Tourism)

Entrance exams for this career

  • CUET UGUniversal · National Testing Agency (NTA)
  • IPMATManagement · IIM Indore

Good things

  • Tourism is a major and growing industry
  • Travel perks and cultural exposure
  • Options as guide, planner, or own travel company

Hard things

  • Seasonal income in some roles
  • Industry is sensitive to global events
