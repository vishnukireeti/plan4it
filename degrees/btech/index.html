B.Tech / B.E. (Bachelor of Technology / Engineering)

Applied mathematics, engineering principles, system design and hands-on technical problem-solving. B.E. leans slightly more toward theoretical fundamentals, while B.Tech emphasizes practical application, though both hold identical industry value in India.
